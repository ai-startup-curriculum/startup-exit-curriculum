## Status — bootstrap session, live postings deferred

Startup Exit & Endgame covers the **transaction-and-endgame branch** of the startup dependency graph — Endgame Strategy & Optionality → M&A Market Structure & Acquirer Motivations → M&A Deal Structures & Consideration → LOI / Negotiation / Definitive Agreements → M&A Diligence & Data-Room Operations → Secondary Transactions & Pre-Exit Liquidity → IPO Readiness & Dual-Track Preparation → IPO Execution (Roadshow, Pricing, Alternatives) → Transaction Governance & Fiduciary Duty → Transaction Communications & Stakeholder Management → Post-Close Integration & 100-Day Plan → Succession & Leadership Transitions → Winding Down (Shutdown Mechanics & Wind-Down Duties) → Post-Transaction Personal & Wealth Planning. Unlike a single-title role, the equivalent live-market postings span several hireable titles across the exit/endgame lifecycle. The live cycle should backfill from:

- **Founder / CEO adjacency:** Founder / CEO of a Series-B → pre-IPO / MATURE startup running a sell-side or IPO process. Filter to postings where the CEO is explicitly named as the endgame owner (sell-side / IPO / succession).
- **Chief Financial Officer (CFO) at growth → pre-IPO** leading M&A or IPO execution — a strict overlap with `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum` demand but scoped to postings where the CFO's transaction responsibilities are load-bearing rather than incidental.
- **General Counsel / VP Legal / Head of Corporate Development / SVP Corporate Development** at Series-C → pre-IPO leading deal execution and integration.
- **Chief of Staff / VP Business Operations** tasked with owning the sell-side or IPO-readiness dual-track.
- **Investment-banking Vice President / Director / Managing Director** on the M&A or ECM (equity capital markets) coverage team for tech / SaaS / healthtech / fintech sponsor coverage groups (Goldman TMT, Morgan Stanley TMT, JPMorgan TMT, Qatalyst, Centerview, Lazard, Evercore, PJT, Moelis, Guggenheim, Raine, LionTree, Union Square Advisors, DBO Partners, Financial Technology Partners).
- **Private-equity / growth-equity Vice President / Principal / Partner** running acquisitions or take-privates (Vista, Thoma Bravo, Silver Lake, TPG, KKR, Apollo, Blackstone Growth, General Atlantic, Warburg Pincus, TA Associates, Bain Capital Tech Opportunities, Francisco Partners, Marlin Equity).
- **Corporate-development leaders at strategic acquirers** (Alphabet, Microsoft, Salesforce, Adobe, Cisco, Oracle, Snowflake, Databricks, ServiceNow, Workday, Palo Alto Networks, Atlassian, CrowdStrike, Datadog) sourcing tuck-in / platform / aqui-hire deals.
- **Interim / Fractional CEO or Turnaround Executive** engaged for a wind-down or sale-for-parts (SierraConstellation, Development Specialists Inc., FTI Consulting, Alvarez & Marsal, AlixPartners, Sherwood Partners for California ABC).
- **Assignment-for-the-Benefit-of-Creditors trustee / restructuring counsel** operating a startup shutdown.

Postings for **pure investment-banking analyst / associate roles whose scope is limited to deal-support mechanics, private-equity operating-partner roles focused only on portfolio-value creation (not transactions), and corporate-development analyst roles focused only on market intelligence** should defer *down* to a future `startup-corporate-development-analyst` sub-track — those cover the mechanic execution layer but not the endgame-owner ownership this track owns.

## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical founder-CEO / CFO / GC / SVP-Corp-Dev / IB-MD task domains at the *pillar* level from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references` — the Delaware corporate-law canon, the NVCA reference documents, the M&A practitioner canon (ABA Deal Points, Cooley, WSGR, Orrick, Latham, Wachtell Lipton, Skadden), the tax framework (IRC 368, 280G, 409A, 1202), the regulatory-approval framework (HSR, CFIUS, EU / UK / China merger control), the SEC and capital-markets framework (S-1, JOBS Act, SPAC rules, direct-listing rules, Nasdaq / NYSE listing standards), the IPO practitioner canon (Davis Polk, Latham, Big Four IPO roadmaps), the secondary + liquidity canon (Nasdaq Private Market, Forge, EquityZen, Carta X, Carta liquidity data, Holloway equity comp), the shutdown / restructuring canon (Bankruptcy Code, WARN, NVCA / Cooley wind-down, Sherwood Partners), the governance canon (Wachtell Lipton takeover law, ISS / Glass Lewis, Woodruff Sawyer D&O, BlackRock / Vanguard stewardship, fairness-opinion practitioner materials), the integration canon (Sirower, McKinsey / BCG / Bain / Big Four PMI), the succession canon (Bolster / Chief / HBR / Charan / Nadler / Wasserman), and the endgame practitioner canon (Horowitz, Thiel, Gurley, Feld, Gil, Hoffman).
2. Anchored the taxonomy against the three-axis model authored in [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) — the [dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md) (transaction-and-endgame branch owned by this repo), the [stage ladder](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) (this pillar becomes actionable at GROWTH → MATURE, with wind-down applicable at any stage), and the founder-numbers slice (never re-taught here).
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the transaction-execution-and-endgame-decision depth for founder-CEO / CFO / GC / SVP-Corp-Dev / IB-MD seats. Everything else defers:
   - *Down* to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the startup-as-a-system model, the graph, the stage axis, the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice. This track *never* re-teaches those.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) (level 40) for the ongoing-company financial infrastructure — three-statement model, cohort unit economics, cap-table maintenance, priced-round preferred-stock economics, board packs, monthly close, finance-org design, and equity *economics* (waterfall math under preference stacks, 409A methodology, Rule 701 aggregate-value caps). Never re-taught here.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) (level 50) for the ongoing-company governance / people / legal / ops infrastructure. This track owns the transaction-specific overlays (special-committee formation, transaction-committee construction, transaction-D&O tail, transaction-communications discipline, transaction-retention plans, transaction-workforce actions under WARN).
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) (level 30) for the ongoing-company GTM infrastructure inspected during buy-side commercial diligence.
   - *Up* to [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), and [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) — role-pathway repos that reference this curriculum rather than re-authoring it. Future `cfo-curriculum`, `coo-curriculum`, `general-counsel-curriculum`, and `head-of-corporate-development-curriculum` will inherit similarly.
   - *Cross-family* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) for the security-engineering content of buy-side diligence, and to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) / [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) for the AI-safety-technical content of buy-side diligence and the AI-vendor-diligence checkpoints.
4. Sized each module against the level-60 depth-and-leadership target (~430 hours total across 14 modules + 3 projects), stage-tagged per [`STARTUP_STAGES.md`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md): mod-101 → GROWTH, mod-102–105 → GROWTH/MATURE, mod-106 → GROWTH, mod-107 → MATURE, mod-108 → MATURE, mod-109 → GROWTH/MATURE, mod-110 → any (transaction-driven), mod-111 → GROWTH/MATURE, mod-112 → MATURE, mod-113 → any (shutdown-triggered), mod-114 → MATURE (post-transaction).
5. Flagged every theme's frequency as `needs-research` so the first live research cycle can populate the number without restructuring the catalog.

## Salary evidence

Not gathered this cycle — WebSearch / WebFetch gated. On the first live cycle, the salary summary should aggregate published US ranges **per equivalent-title bucket AND per company stage** rather than as a single headline aggregate. CFO comp at pre-IPO differs materially from GC comp at Series-C; IB Managing Director comp differs from Corp-Dev SVP comp; turnaround-CEO comp is typically fee-plus-success-based rather than salary-plus-equity; founder-CEO comp in an exit context is dominated by the transaction payout rather than salary. Aggregate at least by:

- Founder / CEO of a Series-B → pre-IPO startup running a sell-side or IPO
- CFO at growth → pre-IPO leading M&A or IPO execution
- General Counsel / VP Legal / Head of Corporate Development / SVP Corporate Development at Series-C → pre-IPO
- Investment-banking VP / Director / Managing Director on the M&A or ECM coverage team for tech
- PE / growth-equity Vice President / Principal / Partner running acquisitions or take-privates
- Corp-Dev leaders at strategic acquirers (tuck-in / platform / aqui-hire deal sourcing)
- Interim / Fractional CEO or Turnaround Executive (retainer + success-fee-based)

Do not quote a headline range until the strict per-bucket-and-stage sample clears **n≥8 with cash + equity + transaction-bonus / success-fee / carry present per cell**. Executive-level comp in an endgame context is almost always cash + equity + performance bonus + transaction bonus + MIP participation; recording only cash would materially understate the total. Fractional / interim-CEO / turnaround-executive comp is usually a monthly retainer plus a success-fee tied to a defined outcome — the live cycle must record which structure was quoted and normalise before aggregating. IB Managing Director comp is base + bonus + long-term incentives with the bonus dominating. PE Partner comp is base + bonus + carry-percent-of-fund. State-level pay-transparency-law-derived ranges (Colorado, California, New York City, Washington, Illinois) are the highest-signal source. Use [BLS OEWS](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/) for Chief Executives / Financial Managers / General Counsel / Business Operations Specialists as the public-benchmark anchor and Carta Total Comp / Pave / Radford / A&M / Heidrick / Spencer Stuart as the private-source anchors.
