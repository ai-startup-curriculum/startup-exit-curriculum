# Resources for mod-103-mna-deal-structures-and-consideration

Primary source documentation, practitioner canon, empirical deal-terms studies, tax and regulatory materials, and case-law references used by this module. Prefer these over secondary write-ups: they are authoritative, and the numeric thresholds in tax and regulatory sections update at least annually — the primary sources are the only reliable version.

> **Numeric thresholds update.** HSR thresholds, filing fees, §280G excise-tax computations, §1202 gross-asset caps and exclusion limits, foreign-antitrust thresholds — all of these change periodically. When an exercise or memo cites a specific number, follow the primary-source link, snapshot the current value with a date, and use that. Do not rely on numbers in secondary literature (including this document) for a live transaction.

## Tax code and regulations

The core Internal Revenue Code sections and Treasury Regulations for this module. All are on the eCFR (regulations) or Cornell Legal Information Institute (statutes).

- **IRC §368 — Definitions relating to corporate reorganizations.** The statutory framework for tax-free reorganizations, including §368(a)(1)(A) statutory merger, §368(a)(1)(B) stock-for-stock, §368(a)(1)(C) stock-for-assets, §368(a)(1)(F) type-F reorganization, §368(a)(2)(D) forward-triangular merger, §368(a)(2)(E) reverse-triangular merger. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/368>
- **Treas. Reg. §1.368-1 through §1.368-3.** Regulations implementing §368, including the continuity-of-interest and continuity-of-business-enterprise doctrines. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1> (search §1.368)
- **IRC §338 — Certain stock purchases treated as asset acquisitions.** The elective asset-deal treatment for qualified stock purchases, including §338(h)(10) joint election for S-corporations and consolidated-group subsidiaries. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/338>
- **Treas. Reg. §1.338(h)(10)-1 and related.** Regulations on the §338(h)(10) election mechanics. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1> (search §1.338)
- **IRC §336(e) — Certain stock sales and distributions may be treated as asset transfers.** A related election for asset-deal treatment where §338(h)(10) is unavailable. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/336>
- **IRC §280G — Golden parachute payments.** The excise-tax and lost-deduction regime for change-of-control compensation. Includes §280G(b)(5) shareholder-vote cleanse for private companies. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/280G>
- **Treas. Reg. §1.280G-1.** The 44 Q&A that govern §280G analysis in practice; the authoritative reference for base-amount computation, parachute-payment identification, and the shareholder-vote cleanse procedure. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/section-1.280G-1>
- **IRC §4999 — Golden parachute payments (excise tax).** The 20% excise tax on excess parachute payments, imposed on the recipient. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4999>
- **IRC §409A — Nonqualified deferred compensation.** The timing-of-payment rules for deferred compensation. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/409A>
- **Treas. Reg. §1.409A-1 through §1.409A-6.** Implementing regulations, including the short-term-deferral exception (§1.409A-1(b)(4)) and the substantial-risk-of-forfeiture concept (§1.409A-1(d)). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1> (search §1.409A)
- **IRC §1202 — Qualified small business stock (QSBS).** The exclusion for gain on QSBS held for at least 5 years. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1202>
- **IRC §1045 — Rollover of gain from qualified small business stock to another qualified small business stock.** The 60-day rollover option for pre-5-year QSBS. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1045>
- **IRC §197 — Amortization of goodwill and certain other intangibles.** The 15-year amortization schedule for stepped-up intangibles in asset deals and §338(h)(10) elections. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/197>
- **IRC §382 — Limitation on net operating loss carryforwards and certain built-in losses following ownership change.** The NOL-limitation regime that applies to acquired corporations. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/382>
- **IRC §7874 — Rules relating to expatriated entities and their foreign parents.** The inversion rules relevant to cross-border transactions. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7874>
- **IRC §351 — Transfer to corporation controlled by transferor.** The tax-free-rollover provision often used for rollover equity in M&A. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/351>
- **IRC §355 — Distribution of stock and securities of a controlled corporation.** The spin-off regime underlying Reverse Morris Trust transactions. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/355>

## IRS guidance and publications

- **IRS Revenue Rulings and Procedures on §368 continuity-of-interest.** Historical Rev. Proc. 77-37 (superseded but instructive) and subsequent guidance on the continuity-of-interest floor. - **IRS Publication 550 — Investment Income and Expenses.** Reference for §1202 and §1045 individual-taxpayer treatment. <https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550>

- **IRS Form 8883 — Asset Allocation Statement Under Section 338.** The asset-allocation form for §338 transactions. <https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8883>

## Empirical deal-terms studies

Empirical practitioner-level studies of what terms actually appear in real M&A transactions — indispensable for calibrating escrow sizing, earn-out prevalence, R&W-insurance uptake, and consideration mix against market practice.

- **ABA Business Law Section — Private Target M&A Deal Points Study.** Biennial empirical study of private-target M&A transactions covering escrow, indemnification, R&W insurance, earn-outs, working-capital adjustments, and material-adverse-change provisions. The shared vocabulary anchor for negotiating M&A deal terms in the sell-side profession. <https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/committees/ma/deal_points/>
- **ABA Business Law Section — Public Target M&A Deal Points Study.** Companion study for public-target transactions.
- **ABA — Strategic Buyer / PE Buyer Deal Points Study.** Comparative study of strategic and financial buyer deal terms.
- **ABA — Canadian Public M&A Deal Points Study.** Cross-border reference for Canadian transactions.
- **ABA — Cross-Border M&A Deal Points Study.** Cross-border M&A term studies.
- **SRS Acquiom — M&A Deal Terms Study (annual).** Annual empirical study of escrow, indemnification, R&W-insurance uptake, earn-out prevalence, working-capital-adjustment mechanics, and closing conditions based on transactions SRS Acquiom administers. One of the highest-signal empirical resources in the M&A profession. <https://www.srsacquiom.com/resources/reports/>
- **AIG / Marsh / Willis Towers Watson / Aon / Woodruff Sawyer / CAC Specialty — R&W insurance market reports.** The R&W insurance market's periodic reports on policy pricing, terms, and claims frequency. Aon: <https://www.aon.com/> Marsh: <https://www.marsh.com/> Willis Towers Watson: <https://www.wtwco.com/> Woodruff Sawyer: <https://woodruffsawyer.com/> CAC Specialty: <https://cacspecialty.com/>

## Delaware Chancery Court — earn-out and structural disputes

Delaware case law on earn-outs, indemnification disputes, working-capital-adjustment disputes, and consideration-design failures.

- **Delaware Court of Chancery — opinions archive.** The primary source for reading current M&A opinions in full. <https://courts.delaware.gov/chancery/>
- **In re Fortis Advisors LLC v. Johnson & Johnson (Del. Ch. 2020)** — earn-out dispute involving Fortis / J&J with substantive review of implied contractual obligations and covenants of good faith. Search for the opinion at courts.delaware.gov.
- **Airborne Health, Inc. v. Squid Soap, LP (Del. Ch. 2010)** — earn-out dispute with instructive framing on "commercially reasonable efforts" standard. Search at courts.delaware.gov.
- **Winshall v. Viacom International Inc. (Del. Ch. 2013)** — earn-out dispute over the definition of the metric and buyer's post-close operational choices. Search at courts.delaware.gov.
- **Chicago Bridge & Iron Co. N.V. v. Westinghouse Electric Company LLC (Del. 2017)** — working-capital-adjustment dispute with substantive review of the boundary between neutral-accountant scope and Chancery adjudication. Search at courts.delaware.gov.
- **Delaware General Corporation Law (DGCL).** The underlying corporate law framework for merger transactions. <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/index.shtml>
- **DGCL §251 — Merger or consolidation of domestic corporations.** The statutory framework for mergers. <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc09/index.shtml>
- **DGCL §262 — Appraisal rights.** Statutory dissenter-rights framework for mergers. <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc09/index.shtml>
- **DGCL §271 — Sale, lease or exchange of assets.** Statutory framework for asset-sale approvals ("all or substantially all"). <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc10/index.shtml>
