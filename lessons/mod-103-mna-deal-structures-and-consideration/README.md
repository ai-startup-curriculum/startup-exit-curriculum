# mod-103 — M&A Deal Structures & Consideration Design

**Estimated effort:** 26 hours
**Stage:** GROWTH / MATURE (the frame installs earlier; the design and negotiation disciplines here are exercised when a specific transaction is drafted — typically inside the 3–9 month window between LOI signing and closing, though the structuring calls that determine the modal outcome are made *before* LOI)
**Seat:** founder-CEO, CFO, GC, SVP-Corp-Dev, tax counsel, M&A counsel, investment-banking VP / Director

This module is where the transaction shape gets designed. mod-101 framed the endgame decision. mod-102 built the market-structure and pre-marketing apparatus that surfaces the buyer set and drives an LOI. mod-103 sits between LOI and closing: the corporate-form choice (asset vs. stock vs. reverse-triangular vs. forward-triangular vs. forward merger vs. Reverse Morris Trust), the consideration mix design (cash vs. stock vs. mixed vs. CVR vs. seller-note holdback vs. rollover equity) against the target's cap-table waterfall, the earn-out design and the dispute mechanics that keep an earn-out out of Delaware Chancery, the escrow / holdback / working-capital-adjustment mechanics against ABA Deal Points Study benchmarks, the tax structuring under IRC §368 and §338(h)(10), the §280G golden-parachute analysis, the §409A payment-timing constraints, the §1202 QSBS preservation choices, and the HSR / CFIUS / foreign-antitrust filing assessment that shapes deal-timing and negotiating leverage.

The module owns the *transaction-structure and consideration-design* layer of sell-side M&A. It does not re-teach cap-table maintenance or waterfall math ([`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) mod-104 owns those, and this module uses the waterfall as a *transaction-input* rather than teaching the mechanic); it does not re-teach LOI-and-definitive-agreement drafting (mod-104 in this track owns exclusivity, no-shop, R&W-and-indemnification package design, MAC/MAE definition, R&W insurance mechanics); it does not re-teach the sell-side data-room or diligence workstream response (mod-105 owns those); and it does not re-teach transaction governance and Delaware fiduciary-duty analysis (mod-109 owns Revlon, Corwin, MFW, special committees, fairness opinions).

## Learning objectives

By the end of this module you should be able to:

- Choose the deal-structure form — asset purchase vs. stock purchase vs. reverse-triangular merger vs. forward-triangular merger vs. forward merger vs. Reverse Morris Trust — and explain the specific reasons the target's founders and the buyer's tax team will disagree on the first pass.
- Design the consideration mix — all-cash vs. all-stock vs. mixed vs. structured (CVRs, seller-note holdback, rollover equity) — against the target's preference-stack waterfall and against the buyer's cash-certainty / equity-upside / tax-treatment trade-off.
- Structure an earn-out — metric selection (revenue, gross-profit, product-milestone, mixed), payout curve (cliff, graduated, accelerated), acquirer control-and-oversight obligations, acceleration-on-breach, and dispute-resolution mechanics — and recognise the failure modes that produce Delaware Chancery litigation.
- Design escrow / holdback / working-capital-adjustment mechanics — escrow sizing and survival against ABA Deal Points Study benchmarks, specific-vs-general indemnity buckets, working-capital true-up definition, closing-cash and closing-debt handling.
- Structure the tax treatment — IRC §368(a)(1)(A) statutory merger, §368(a)(1)(B) stock-for-stock, §368(a)(1)(C) stock-for-assets, §368(a)(1)(F) type-F reorg for the Delaware LLC drop-down pattern, §338(h)(10) election that treats a stock deal as an asset deal for tax, taxable stock, taxable asset — and choose the structure that fits the target's entity type and shareholder mix.
- Apply IRC §280G golden-parachute analysis — identify disqualified individuals, compute the base amount, identify parachute payments and their 3x threshold, model the 20% excise tax and lost-corporate-deduction impact, and design mitigations (private-company §280G(b)(5) shareholder vote, cutback provisions, value-shifting into reasonable-comp buckets, pre-close bonus payments outside the change-of-control window).
- Apply IRC §409A payment-timing constraints to earn-outs and transaction bonuses — the permissible-payment-event constraints, the short-term-deferral exception, and the substantial-risk-of-forfeiture window that keeps an earn-out compliant.
- Preserve IRC §1202 QSBS treatment through the transaction — the 5-year holding period a stock deal can preserve and an asset deal cannot, the §1045 rollover option, the QSBS-preservation criterion for founder-and-early-employee-preserving deals, and the interaction with §368 tax-free reorg treatment.
- Assess regulatory-filing implications — HSR filing (current threshold ~$126.4M in 2025, updated annually by the FTC), HSR filing-fee tiers, HSR waiting-period mechanics (30 days initial, plus a Second Request that stops the clock), CFIUS national-security review (foreign acquirer, sensitive technology, critical infrastructure, sensitive data / TID US business), foreign-antitrust filings (EU Merger Regulation, UK CMA, China SAMR, other jurisdictions) — and translate filing exposure into deal-timing and negotiating-leverage implications.

## Ownership boundary

This module owns the transaction-structure and consideration-design layer. It does *not* re-teach:

- **Cap-table maintenance, waterfall math, preferred-stock economics, 409A methodology for equity-grant valuation, ISO / NSO / RSU instrument economics** — `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum` mod-104 (equity economics) owns those. This module *uses* the waterfall as a transaction-input; when you need to compute who receives what under a $500M all-cash sale of a company with a $180M preference stack, that mechanic is imported from mod-104 rather than re-derived here.
- **LOI-and-definitive-agreement authoring** — exclusivity, no-shop, standstill, expense-reimbursement, break-up fee, matching-rights, the reps-and-warranties layering, interim-operating covenants, closing conditions, MAC / MAE definition negotiation, the sole-and-exclusive-remedy / sandbagging language, R&W-insurance-vs-traditional-indemnification structuring — mod-104 in this track owns those. This module chooses the *structure and mechanics*; mod-104 negotiates and drafts them.
- **Sell-side data-room construction and diligence-workstream response** — mod-105 owns those.
- **Transaction governance and Delaware fiduciary-duty analysis at deal-scale** — Revlon, Corwin, MFW, entire-fairness review, special-committee construction, fairness-opinion procurement, Rural Metro banker-conflict analysis, DGCL §262 appraisal exposure — mod-109 owns those. This module surfaces the structuring calls that later become fiduciary-duty questions (a founder-favouring rollover; a management-favouring earn-out; a §280G shareholder-vote sequencing) but does not adjudicate the fiduciary analysis.
- **Transaction communications — pre-signing and post-signing employee, customer, press, and analyst choreography; management-incentive-plan (MIP) design as a communications artefact; WARN Act compliance** — mod-110 owns those. This module designs the MIP's *§280G-safe economics* and the retention structure's *§409A-compliant timing*; mod-110 rolls out the communications.
- **Post-close integration — escrow release choreography, earn-out achievement operations, working-capital true-up execution** — mod-111 owns those. This module designs the mechanics that mod-111 later administers.
- **Post-transaction personal-wealth planning — QSBS stacking, §1045 rollover execution mechanics, 10b5-1 plans, family-office construction** — mod-114 owns those. This module preserves QSBS treatment as a *structuring criterion*; mod-114 develops the personal-wealth optimisation on the preserved treatment.

What this module *adds* on top: the corporate-form decision framework that determines what the transaction can legally be, the consideration-design discipline that reasons across cash-certainty / equity-upside / tax-treatment / waterfall-fairness, the earn-out design toolkit calibrated against the specific dispute failure modes that recur, the escrow-and-adjustment mechanics benchmarked against practitioner data, the tax-structuring toolkit across §368 / §338(h)(10) / §280G / §409A / §1202, and the regulatory-filing assessment framework that translates HSR / CFIUS / foreign-antitrust exposure into deal-timing consequences.

## How to work through this module

Carry a specific hypothetical transaction through the module — either an extension of the mod-101/mod-102 buyer-map company (recommended, so the artefacts stack), or a fresh fact pattern. Fix the target profile (entity type, cap table, preference stack, revenue, historical financials for the base-amount calculation, jurisdiction, foreign-shareholder mix), fix the acquirer profile (public / private, entity type, acquirer stock currency and price, cash on balance sheet), and fix the headline number and rough structure the LOI proposes. Every exercise refines the structure against the fixed profile.

Chapters 1–4 install the transactional mechanics — corporate form, consideration mix, earn-outs, escrow and adjustments. Chapters 5–8 install the tax-code framework — §368 / §338(h)(10) tax-free-vs-taxable structure, §280G parachutes, §409A timing, §1202 QSBS. Chapter 9 installs the regulatory-filing framework — HSR, CFIUS, foreign antitrust. Read a chapter, then do the matching exercise while the frame is fresh. The chapters are ordered so that structuring (1–4) precedes tax (5–8) precedes regulatory (9), but in a live transaction all three tracks run in parallel with weekly cross-referencing — the tax-optimal structure may fail the HSR threshold analysis, and the consideration mix that maximises seller QSBS preservation may fail the buyer's §280G shareholder-vote sequencing. The module teaches each track in isolation so that in practice you can hold the interactions.
