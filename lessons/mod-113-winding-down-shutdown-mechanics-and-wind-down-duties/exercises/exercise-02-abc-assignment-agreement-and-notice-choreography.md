## Starter guidance

Three anti-patterns to avoid:

- **"The assignee will run everything so we don't need to prepare."** The assignee runs the specific post-signing execution, but the specific pre-signing workstream (assignee selection; assignment-agreement terms; notice choreography; pay-order discipline; D&O tail; WARN notice) is the specific board's responsibility. An unprepared board walks into the specific assignment with specific gaps that specifically cost the specific estate.
- **"ABC is like Chapter 7 but faster."** Only partially. ABC is specifically faster and specifically cheaper, but the specific creditor-recovery and specific D&O exposure signatures are specifically different. ABC specifically does not deliver the specific statutory automatic stay of §362 or the specific "free and clear" §363(f) machinery. If specific creditors are specifically threatening specific collection or if specific IP encumbrances specifically require specific "free and clear" title, ABC is specifically wrong.
- **"We can just pay creditors pro rata."** No. The specific priority waterfall is specific and must be followed. Specific trust-fund tax and specific wage obligations must be specifically paid in full before specific general unsecured receives anything. Specifically "pro rata" across all creditors specifically violates the pay-first discipline (chapter 9).

Three operational suggestions:

- **Engage the assignee candidate before engaging restructuring counsel's drafting workstream.** The specific assignee has specific drafting conventions and specific preferences; engaging the specific assignee first lets the specific drafting work with the specific template rather than against it.
- **Build the specific creditor list with specific proof-of-claim mailing information before the assignment-agreement signing.** The specific notice workstream is specific logistics (specific physical addresses; specific registered-agent information; specific electronic-delivery consents) that is specifically time-consuming and specifically must be ready at the specific signing date.
- **Coordinate the specific pay-order discipline with the specific assignment-agreement signing.** Specific trust-fund tax deposits and specific wage payments should be specifically made from the specific pre-signing company cash, not from the specific post-signing trust estate — the specific cleanest allocation specifically defuses specific later challenges.
