# Resources for mod-113-winding-down-shutdown-mechanics-and-wind-down-duties

Primary-source statutes, bankruptcy-court opinions, state ABC frameworks, practitioner memoranda, assignee-firm published methodology, broker canon, and regulatory guidance used by this module. Prefer these over secondary write-ups: the statutes are the authoritative text; the opinions shape the operating doctrine; the assignee-firm and practitioner publications are the operating-practice canon that specifically reads live.

> **Live doctrine disclaimer.** The specific statutes and regulations below evolve. The 2024 FTC non-compete rule, the specific state wage-and-hour reforms, the specific Delaware fiduciary-duty jurisprudence, and the specific state ABC statutory frameworks all specifically shift. Every citation below is a specific starting point; specific verify current status before relying on any specific framing.

## US Bankruptcy Code

  - **Chapter 1 — General Provisions.** Specific definitions in 11 U.S.C. §101.
  - **Chapter 3 — Case Administration.** The specific automatic stay (§362); specific trustee / examiner provisions.
  - **Chapter 5 — Creditors, Debtor, and Estate.** The specific priority of claims (§507); specific property of the estate (§541); specific avoiding powers (§§544, 547, 548); specific sales (§363).
