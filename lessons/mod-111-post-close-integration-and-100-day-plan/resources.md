# Resources for mod-111-post-close-integration-and-100-day-plan

Primary-source accounting standards, regulatory references, practitioner canon, and vendor documentation covering the specific post-close integration overlay this module teaches. Prefer the primary sources (FASB, SEC, ONC, cloud-vendor well-architected frameworks, agent-published studies) when a question is operational; the Big-Four / strategy-consultancy and specific practitioner-canon publications help calibrate current market practice but are secondary to the specific contractual, accounting, and regulatory primary sources that govern specific transactions.

> **This is education, not legal, tax, accounting, employment, or systems-integration advice.** Every live integration engages qualified counsel (corporate M&A, tax, employment, IP, benefits), the acquirer's audit firm for post-close finance-consolidation questions, HR-specialist advisors for benefits and comp harmonisation, IT-integration and identity consultants, and — for the specific escrow / earn-out workstream — the specific escrow agent and (if R&W-insured) the specific R&W broker and underwriter named in the closing documents. The materials below are what a competent integration team would read; they are not a substitute for engagement of advisors.

> **Practitioner publications evolve.** Big-Four PMI frameworks, strategy-consultancy publications, SRS Acquiom and ABA deal-terms studies, HRIS and IdP vendor documentation, and specific cloud-vendor migration tooling all update regularly. Treat every URL below as a starting point for reading the specific current text, not a frozen reference.
