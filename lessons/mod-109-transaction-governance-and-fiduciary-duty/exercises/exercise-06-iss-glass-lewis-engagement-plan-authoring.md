# exercise-06: ISS / Glass Lewis Engagement Plan Authoring

**Estimated effort:** 4 hours

## Objective

Author the full **proxy-advisor engagement plan** for the specific transaction stockholder vote (M&A merger vote, going-private vote, or IPO-governance-graduation vote). By the end you should have a written stakeholder map, a pre-engagement discipline schedule, a response-to-recommendation playbook, and a specific vote-outcome scenario analysis that a CFO and head of IR can execute against.

## Background

This exercise covers material from:

- [Chapter 8 — ISS, Glass Lewis, and Stockholder-Litigation Defence](../08-iss-glass-lewis-and-litigation-defence.md)

Proxy advisors — Institutional Shareholder Services (ISS) and Glass Lewis — issue formal voting recommendations to their institutional-investor clients ahead of the stockholder vote. For an M&A transaction, the recommendation is "For," "Against," or (less common) "Abstain / Caution." For a public issuer with meaningful index-fund / managed-passive-money concentration (typically 25–40%+ of the float on a mid-to-large-cap issuer), the recommendation is directly outcome-determinative: a specific "For" from ISS and Glass Lewis moves specific index-fund votes with the recommendation; a specific "Against" moves specific index-fund votes against the deal. The specific proxy-advisor engagement discipline is the specific pre-vote workstream that influences the recommendation and, failing that, prepares the specific response letter that is itself a disclosed proxy supplement.

## Prerequisites

- The exercise-01 through exercise-05 outputs — the standards-of-review analysis, committee construction, fairness-opinion packet, banker-conflict analysis, and appraisal exposure.
- The target's current institutional-holder breakdown. For a public target, specifically the 13F / NPX filings of the top 25 institutional holders; specifically whether each is an index-fund (BlackRock, Vanguard, State Street — specific passive-indexed component), a specific active holder, or a specific hedge-fund / arbitrage holder.
- Access to the ISS and Glass Lewis policy documents: ISS United States Proxy Voting Guidelines (freely available via <https://www.issgovernance.com/policy-gateway/voting-policies/>), ISS Benchmark Policy Updates, and Glass Lewis Policy Guidelines (freely available via <https://www.glasslewis.com/voting-policies-current/>).
- Access to the specific `DEFM14A` filings of comparable transactions, so that you can read the specific "Response to Recommendation" supplements that specific issuers have filed.

## Tasks

### 1. Institutional-holder map and voting-power concentration

For the specific target's top 25 institutional holders, produce a specific map:

- **Holder identity.** Named institution (BlackRock, Vanguard, State Street, Fidelity, Capital Group / American Funds, T. Rowe Price, Wellington, Dodge & Cox, specific hedge-fund / arbitrage holder).
- **Specific holding.** Shares held, percentage of outstanding, as of specific record date.
- **Specific vote type.** Index-fund (follows ISS or Glass Lewis mechanically with specific override exceptions), active-managed (does own analysis but weighs ISS / Glass Lewis materially), specific-strategy (hedge fund, event-driven, arbitrage — may or may not follow proxy advisor).
- **Specific voting history.** Historical pattern on M&A votes at other issuers — specific overall approval rate, specific "against" record on specific types of deals.
- **Specific stewardship team.** Named head of corporate governance / stewardship for the specific institution. BlackRock Investment Stewardship, Vanguard Investment Stewardship, State Street Asset Stewardship, Fidelity Stewardship — specific named contacts.
- **Specific pre-engagement history.** Any specific prior engagement with the target or the specific investor-relations team.

Aggregate: what percentage of outstanding is specifically ISS-recommendation-sensitive (index-fund + specifically-follows-ISS active)? What percentage is specifically Glass-Lewis-sensitive? What percentage is specifically independent of proxy-advisor recommendation?

### 2. ISS and Glass Lewis policy-mapping memo

Walk the specific ISS and Glass Lewis policies relevant to the specific transaction type. For each:

- **M&A-specific policy.** ISS M&A policy (ISS Benchmark US Proxy Voting Guidelines, Section 4 — Capital Structure, Mergers and Acquisitions). Specific case-by-case approach, specific factors considered (valuation, strategic rationale, process, management conflicts, governance, specific director conduct). Glass Lewis M&A policy (Glass Lewis US Proxy Paper Guidelines, Section on M&A). Specific comparable case-by-case approach with specific explicit factors.
- **Going-private-specific policy.** ISS and Glass Lewis specifically apply enhanced scrutiny to going-private transactions, with specific attention to MFW compliance, specific banker conflicts (post-*Rural Metro*), specific premium magnitude, specific minority-protection terms.
- **IPO-governance-graduation-specific policy.** ISS classified-board policy (ISS specifically votes against classified boards post-IPO at most issuers); specific dual-class-share policy (ISS specifically supports proposals to eliminate dual-class and specifically votes against directors at dual-class issuers over time, with sunset-provision tolerance); specific anti-takeover-mechanism policies (poison pills, supermajority provisions, written-consent restrictions, specific advance-notice bylaws). Glass Lewis specifically has parallel positions with specific-detail differences.
- **Compensation-related aspects.** ISS Insight M&A module and Insight Compensation module specifically cover the "golden parachute" vote (DGCL requires a specific advisory vote on executive-change-in-control compensation under Dodd-Frank). ISS and Glass Lewis specifically evaluate parachute magnitude, tax-gross-up provisions, specific accelerated-vesting treatment.
- **Specific disclosure-based policy.** ISS and Glass Lewis specifically expect specific proxy disclosure quality — specifically the "Background of the Merger" narrative, specifically the "Opinion of the Financial Advisor" valuation detail (chapter 6 exercise-03), specifically the banker-conflict disclosure (chapter 5 exercise-04). Specific policy-stated expectations are documented.

For each specific policy, produce a specific "my transaction evaluated" line — does my transaction specifically pass the policy test, does it specifically fail, does it specifically require specific proxy supplement to pass.

### 3. Pre-engagement calendar

Draft a specific pre-engagement calendar covering the window from signing to vote (typical 60-90 days for a long-form merger with full proxy process):

- **T-60 (signing + 1 day).** Specific initial-engagement call with specific ISS M&A analyst and specific Glass Lewis research analyst — presentation of transaction rationale, specific board process, specific banker engagement, specific valuation summary.
- **T-45 (proxy filing).** Specific definitive-proxy filing with specific comprehensive narrative, specific valuation detail, specific conflicts disclosures. Delivery to ISS / Glass Lewis.
- **T-35 to T-25.** Specific window during which ISS / Glass Lewis conduct their analysis. Specific response-to-questions discipline — specific IR / counsel / banker available to answer specific questions. Specific "no new information outside the proxy" discipline.
- **T-25 to T-20 (recommendations issue).** ISS and Glass Lewis typically issue specific recommendations 20-30 days before the stockholder meeting (ISS somewhat later than Glass Lewis is common; the specific timing varies).
- **T-20 to T-10.** Specific "respond to recommendation" window if the recommendation is "Against" or "Caution." Draft the specific response-to-ISS letter or press release / proxy supplement.
- **T-10 to T-5.** Specific stockholder-outreach-and-education — specific IR calls to specific top-25 holders, specific proxy-solicitation-firm engagement (D.F. King, MacKenzie Partners, Morrow Sodali, Innisfree M&A, Georgeson). Specific "get out the vote" discipline.
- **T-5 to T-0.** Specific final-tabulation / vote-advance-indicator monitoring; specific prepared-remarks for specific vote announcement; specific contingency for specifically-close vote outcomes.

### 4. Engagement-call briefing book

Draft the specific briefing book for the initial-engagement call with each of ISS and Glass Lewis. Required elements:

- **Executive summary** of the transaction. 1 page — parties, consideration, specific premium, specific strategic rationale, specific process.
- **Standards-of-review posture.** Specific summary of the exercise-01 analysis — governing standard (Revlon? Corwin cleansing targeted? MFW?), specific board actions.
- **Board process.** Specific committee construction (if any), specific advisor selection, specific meeting cadence, specific competing-process consideration (go-shop? Market check?).
- **Valuation.** Specific valuation ranges from the fairness opinion, specific DCF detail, specific precedent-transactions detail, specific premium analysis.
- **Specific interested-party disclosure.** Specific directors / officers with specific transaction-contingent compensation. Specific controller status if any.
- **Banker conflicts.** Specific exercise-04 output — specific past relationships, specific fee structure, specific stapled-financing if any.
- **Specific vote architecture.** Specific majority-of-outstanding or specific majority-of-votes-cast; specific majority-of-minority if MFW; specific voting-agreement coverage.
- **Specific "why we are a Corwin / MFW cleanse" argument** or specific "why Revlon applies and we have run a reasonable process" argument.
- **Specific anticipated-question handling.** Specific prepared answers to specifically-expected questions on process, valuation, conflicts, parachute compensation, dissent exposure.

### 5. Response-to-recommendation letter draft

Draft the specific response-to-recommendation letter in two versions:

- **Version A.** Response to a specific ISS "Against" recommendation. Specifically identify the specific ISS concern (process gap, valuation gap, specific conflict, specific disclosure). Specifically rebut each concern with specific evidence from the proxy or specifically supplement the proxy with additional disclosure. Specific tone — respectful, specific-fact-based, not combative. Length: 2-4 pages.
- **Version B.** Response to a specific Glass Lewis "Against" recommendation. Specific parallel treatment, specifically tailored to the specifically-identified Glass Lewis concerns (which may differ from ISS even on the same transaction).

The response-letter is typically filed as a proxy supplement under Rule 14a-6 and included in the specific stockholder-outreach discipline.

### 6. Vote-outcome scenario analysis

Model specific vote-outcome scenarios with specific numbers:

- **Base case.** ISS "For" / Glass Lewis "For" / specific index-fund-pass-through at 100% ISS / specific active-manager-split 60/40 for / specific hedge-fund-split 50/50. Specific implied approval percentage: specific percentage.
- **Downside case.** ISS "Against" / Glass Lewis "Against" / specific index-fund-pass-through at 100% ISS / specific active-manager-split 30/70 for / specific hedge-fund-split 30/70 for. Specific implied approval percentage.
- **Mixed case.** ISS "Against" / Glass Lewis "For" (or vice versa) / specific implied approval percentage.
- **Specific majority-of-minority stress test** (if MFW): remove the specific controller stake and specific affiliated stake from the denominator; what percentage of the specific minority specifically approves in each scenario?
- **Specific break-point.** At what specific approval percentage does the merger agreement require re-pricing or re-signing? Specific minimum-vote closing condition if any.

Produce a specific recommendation to the board on specific contingency actions:

- Specific additional proxy supplement if specific recommendation issues require it.
- Specific stockholder outreach budget beyond standard specific proxy-solicitor engagement.
- Specific voting-agreement extension / specific side-letter with specifically-known holders.
- Specific merger-agreement amendment if specific vote breaks the condition.

## Starter guidance

Three anti-patterns to avoid:

- **The "we have a great deal; the proxy advisors will see that".** Proxy advisors specifically read the specific proxy — the "Background of the Merger," the specific valuation detail, the specific conflicts, the specific director-process. A thin proxy specifically earns a specific "Against" recommendation regardless of the specific merits. The specific pre-engagement discipline and the specific proxy quality are the specific inputs the advisors react to.
- **The "we'll fight ISS in the press".** The specific response-to-recommendation letter is a legal-process document — specifically filed, specifically subject to Rule 14a-9 anti-misstatement liability. A specifically combative tone or specifically unsupported counter-claim is a specific litigation risk. Specific-fact-based responses are the specific norm.
- **The "we'll call the ISS senior-leadership and get this reversed".** ISS and Glass Lewis specifically do not reverse recommendations outside their specific process. Specific escalation to specific senior staff is appropriate on specific factual-error issues, but specific "I want you to change your view" conversations are specifically counterproductive and specifically are documented in a way that specifically appears in subsequent-year recommendations.

## Acceptance criteria

You can demonstrate that:

- The institutional-holder map covers the top 25 holders with specific holding-size, specific vote-type, specific stewardship contact.
- The ISS / Glass Lewis policy-mapping memo evaluates specific policy sections against specific transaction facts with specific findings.
- The pre-engagement calendar covers the full T-60 to T-0 window with specific-day actions.
- The engagement-call briefing book covers executive summary, standards-of-review, board process, valuation, interested-party disclosure, banker conflicts, vote architecture, and specific-anticipated-question handling.
- The response-to-recommendation letter is drafted in both ISS "Against" and Glass Lewis "Against" versions.
- The vote-outcome scenario analysis has specific numbers (not placeholder values).
- A critical reader (head of IR, proxy solicitor, peer general counsel, specific institutional-stewardship contact) could stress-test a specific assumption and see the specific evidence you used.
