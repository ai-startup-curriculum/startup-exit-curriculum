# Resources for mod-110-transaction-communications-and-stakeholder-management

Primary-source statutes, regulations, and regulator-published guidance covering the specific communications, compensation, and workforce-action overlays this module teaches, plus the leading practitioner canon used by deal-team principals and transaction counsel. Prefer the primary sources when a question is operational; the practitioner commentary helps calibrate current market practice but is secondary to the statutes and the regulator guidance.

> **Jurisdiction and regulation evolution.** The specific WARN, works-council, merger-control, CFIUS, Reg FD, and §280G / §409A frameworks evolve. State mini-WARN statutes amend regularly (New Jersey's 2023 amendments are the specific recent example). SEC and FINRA rules are updated by rulemaking. ISS and Glass Lewis policies are revised annually. Treat every URL below as a starting point for reading the specific current text, not a frozen reference. Verify against current regulatory text and current practitioner commentary before relying on any specific framing in a live transaction.

> **This is education, not legal, tax, employment, or communications advice.** Every live transaction engages qualified securities counsel, executive-compensation counsel, employment counsel, communications / PR firms, and (for cross-border transactions) local jurisdictional counsel. The materials below are what a competent deal team would read; they are not a substitute for engagement of counsel.
