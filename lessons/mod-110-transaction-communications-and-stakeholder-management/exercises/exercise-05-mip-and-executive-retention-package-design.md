## Starter guidance

Three anti-patterns to avoid:

- **"The accountants will do the §280G calculation at close."** Specifically correct — but the specific *design* of the specific overlay cannot wait for the specific close-time calculation. The specific calculation must be specifically run specifically pre-signing on the specific proposed terms with specific sensitivity analysis.
- **"Gross-ups are standard."** Specifically not post-Dodd-Frank. The specific ISS / Glass Lewis policies specifically flag specific gross-ups as a specific problematic practice, and specific institutional-investor pressure specifically drove specific most public companies to specifically eliminate specific gross-ups from specific new agreements. Specific legacy gross-ups persist in specific legacy agreements; specific new arrangements typically use the specific best-net cutback. Specific private companies have specific more flexibility but typically still avoid gross-ups on specific tax-efficiency grounds.
- **"§409A is a drafting issue — our comp counsel handles it."** Specifically correct — but the specific business-term design (specific payment timing, specific tranching, specific CIC-trigger versus specific service-trigger, specific earn-out structure) specifically determines whether the specific arrangement is specifically §409A-compliant in the first place. The specific CFO / specific GC needs to understand the specific §409A implications of the specific business-term design, not just rely on the specific drafting fix.
