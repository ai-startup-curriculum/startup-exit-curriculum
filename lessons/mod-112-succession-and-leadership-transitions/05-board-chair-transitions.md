The specific pattern is not universal — many high-performing companies (Apple through Steve Jobs's tenure, Meta / Facebook, Alphabet historically, many PE-portfolio companies) have retained combined-chair-and-CEO structures — but the specific default has shifted in the mature-stage governance environment toward independent chair.
