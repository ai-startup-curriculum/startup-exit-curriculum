# Resources for mod-105-mna-diligence-and-data-room-operations

Primary source documentation, practitioner-grade references, empirical deal-terms studies, VDR-vendor documentation, QoE-provider references, R&W-insurance market reports, OSS-licence references, AI-governance primary sources, and the diligence-canon writing used by this module. Prefer these over secondary write-ups: the empirical studies refresh at least annually, the regulatory text is the authoritative statement of the rule, and the practitioner memos are the fastest-updating read on current market practice.

> **Benchmarks refresh.** Numeric practitioner benchmarks cited in this module — diligence-response SLA windows, R&W retention percentages, premium rates on line, workstream durations, provider fee ranges, price-adjustment magnitudes — are drawn from published studies and broker reports that update annually. When an exercise or memo cites a specific number, follow the primary-source link, snapshot the current value with a date, and use that. Do not rely on numbers in secondary literature (including this document) for a live transaction.

> **VDR feature sets update.** Virtual-data-room product features (Intralinks, Datasite, Firmex, Ansarada, DealRoom, iDeals, ShareVault) update at each vendor's release cadence. Verify the current-generation capability (permissioning granularity, redaction tooling, Q&A workflow, audit-log detail, AI-assisted classification, mobile access, SSO, API) against the vendor's documentation before authoring a comparison for a live matter.

> **Regulatory text is primary.** The EU AI Act, EU GDPR, US state-privacy statutes, HIPAA, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, HSR thresholds, and the various sector-specific regulatory regimes change on their own cadence. Follow the primary-source link; do not rely on a secondary write-up for a live-transaction compliance determination.

> **This is education, not legal, tax, accounting, or insurance advice.** A live transaction hands off to qualified M&A counsel, a QoE provider, an R&W broker, specialist IP / privacy / AI / regulatory counsel, and an audit firm. The intent of this document is to point to the materials a competent CFO, GC, Corp-Dev VP, or M&A partner would read; it is not a substitute for engagement of those professionals.

## Empirical deal-terms studies

The two most-cited empirical sources on US private-target M&A deal terms, with the diligence- and R&W-specific chapters repeatedly cited in this module.

- **ABA Business Law Section — Private Target Mergers & Acquisitions Deal Points Study.** Published periodically (typically every two years) by the ABA M&A Market Trends Subcommittee. The diligence-adjacent chapters cover rep scope / qualifiers, indemnification cap and basket, R&W-insurance usage trends, disclosure-schedule practice, and specific-indemnity carve-out prevalence. <https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/market-trends/>
- **ABA Business Law Section — Strategic Buyer / Private Equity Buyer Deal Points Study.** Strategic-vs-PE differences in diligence intensity, R&W placement, and seller-indemnification architecture. <https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/market-trends/>
- **SRS Acquiom — M&A Deal Terms Study (annual).** SRS acts as shareholder representative on several hundred private-target transactions per year; the annual study is the practitioner reference for escrow sizing, R&W-insurance usage, claim frequency and severity, and the post-closing dispute pattern. <https://www.srsacquiom.com/resources/>
- **SRS Acquiom — Buy-Side Representations & Warranties Insurance Study.** Primary empirical source on R&W-insurance claims, denial rates, and exclusion-triggered disputes. The clearest reference for understanding how specific diligence findings become specific exclusions and specific post-close claims. <https://www.srsacquiom.com/resources/>
- **SRS Acquiom — Escrow and Indemnification Study.** Escrow sizes, claim frequency, release timing, and resolution-outcome distribution. <https://www.srsacquiom.com/resources/>
- **American Bar Association — Model Stock Purchase Agreement with Commentary (Private Target).** The ABA's annotated model SPA; the commentary on the rep-set and the disclosure-schedule architecture is especially useful as a disciplined reference for what the diligence workstreams ultimately feed. <https://www.americanbar.org/products/inv/book/>

## Accounting standards

The accounting standards that anchor financial-, QoE-, and purchase-price-mechanic diligence.

- **FASB Accounting Standards Codification (ASC).** The authoritative US GAAP codification. Each ASC topic is linked below. <https://asc.fasb.org/> (subscription required for full text; summaries are free)
- **ASC 606 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers.** The five-step revenue-recognition model that governs the sell-side-and-buy-side QoE revenue analysis. <https://asc.fasb.org/606>
- **ASC 842 — Leases.** Right-of-use and lease-liability accounting; relevant to the real-estate and debt-like-items analysis. <https://asc.fasb.org/842>
- **ASC 350-40 — Internal-Use Software.** Capitalised-software-development-cost accounting. <https://asc.fasb.org/350>
- **ASC 740 — Income Taxes (incl. uncertain tax positions).** The uncertain-tax-position memo anchor for tax-diligence review. <https://asc.fasb.org/740>
- **ASC 805 — Business Combinations.** Purchase-accounting rules including the fair-value treatment of acquired deferred revenue (and its subsequent modification in ASU 2021-08). <https://asc.fasb.org/805>
- **FASB ASU 2021-08 — Accounting for Contract Assets and Contract Liabilities from Contracts with Customers.** The 2021 update that aligns an acquirer's post-close revenue recognition from acquired contracts to the target's pre-close presentation. The reference for the QoE's deferred-revenue walk under current practice. <https://fasb.org/page/document?pdf=ASU2021-08.pdf>
- **PCAOB Audit Standards.** The audit standards that apply to the target's audited financials when a Big Four or regional firm has performed the audit. <https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards>
- **ASC 820 — Fair Value Measurement.** Fair-value determination for assets and liabilities, relevant to purchase accounting and the QoE's debt-like-items valuation. <https://asc.fasb.org/820>
