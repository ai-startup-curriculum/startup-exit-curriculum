# Resources for mod-108-ipo-execution-roadshow-pricing-and-alternatives

Primary source documentation, practitioner canon, exchange rulebooks, and data providers used by this module. The IPO execution regime is rule-dense — Section 5 of the Securities Act, the JOBS Act TTW framework, Regulation M, the FINRA Rules 2242 / 5110 / 5130 / 5131, the exchange direct-listing frameworks, and the SEC's 2024 SPAC rules — and the sources below are the authoritative texts the working group and counsel will cite in live diligence.

## Securities Act and Exchange Act framework

- **Securities Act of 1933** — the specific statutory text. Section 2(a)(3) (definition of "offer"), Section 5 (gun-jumping prohibitions), Section 5(d) (JOBS Act testing-the-waters privilege, as amended), Section 6(e) (confidential-DRS submissions by EGCs), Section 11 (civil liability for registration-statement misstatements), Section 12(a)(1) (rescission liability for Section 5 violations), Section 12(a)(2) (seller liability for prospectus-based misstatements), Section 15 (control-person liability), Section 27A (PSLRA safe harbour for forward-looking statements). <https://www.sec.gov/about/laws/sa33.pdf>
- **Securities Exchange Act of 1934** — the specific statutory text, including Section 10(b) and Rule 10b-5 anti-fraud, Section 13 (periodic reporting), Section 16 (insider-reporting and short-swing-profit), Section 21E (PSLRA safe harbour for Exchange Act filings). <https://www.sec.gov/about/laws/sea34.pdf>
- **SEC EDGAR** — the source of truth for all S-1 filings, Rule 424(b) pricing prospectuses, Form 8-K greenshoe-exercise disclosures, Form S-4 / F-4 de-SPAC registration statements, Form 1-A Reg A+ offerings, and all post-effective reporting. <https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search-and-access>
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance — Compliance and Disclosure Interpretations (C&DIs)** — specifically the Securities Act Sections and Rules, Securities Act Forms, and Exchange Act Sections C&DIs addressing Section 5(d) TTW, Rule 433 FWPs, Rule 134 / 135 announcements, Rule 163A pre-filing safe harbour, Rule 163B TTW extension, and the 2024 SPAC rules. <https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual>
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance — Financial Reporting Manual (FRM)** — specific accounting-and-disclosure interpretations relevant to pricing-prospectus content, pro-forma presentations under Rule 11-02 of Reg S-X, and de-SPAC transaction accounting. <https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual>

- **Rule 137 / Rule 138 / Rule 139 — research-report safe harbours (17 CFR 230.137–139)** — the specific research-publication safe harbours applicable during the offering window. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230>

- **JOBS Act (Jumpstart Our Business Startups Act of 2012, Pub. L. No. 112-106)** — the specific statutory text, including Section 105(c) adding Section 5(d) TTW and Section 106(a) adding Section 6(e) confidential-DRS. <https://www.congress.gov/bill/112th-congress/house-bill/3606>

## Pricing, allocation, and FINRA new-issue-allocation rules

- **Rule 430A — pricing supplements and pricing outside the range (17 CFR 230.430A)** — the specific framework for pricing outside the last-filed range, including the 20%-spread convention. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230/section-230.430A>
- **Rule 424(b) — final-prospectus filing (17 CFR 230.424)**. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230/section-230.424>
- **Rule 462(b) — effectiveness amendments for pricing (17 CFR 230.462)**. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230/section-230.462>
- **FINRA Rule 5110 — public-offering fairness and reasonableness** — governs underwriting compensation (gross spread, reimbursable expenses, warrants, specific-ancillary-arrangements) and the specific pre-pricing FINRA filing. <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5110>
- **FINRA Rule 5121 — public offerings of securities with conflicts of interest** — governs offerings where the underwriter has a specified conflict of interest with the issuer. <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5121>
- **FINRA Rule 5130 — restrictions on the purchase and sale of IPOs of equity securities** — "restricted persons" cannot buy IPO allocations. <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5130>
- **FINRA Rule 5131 — new-issue allocations and distributions** — the anti-spinning rule and specific allocation disclosure. <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/5131>
- **FINRA Rule 2242 — research analysts and research reports** — the specific analyst-conflict-of-interest rule including the post-pricing quiet-period discipline for syndicate-member research. <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2242>
