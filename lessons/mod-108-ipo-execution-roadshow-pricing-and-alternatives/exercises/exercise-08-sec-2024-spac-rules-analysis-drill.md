# exercise-08: SEC 2024 SPAC Rules Analysis Drill

**Estimated effort:** 3 hours

## Objective

Perform a close reading of the SEC's January 2024 SPAC rules (Release Nos. 33-11265, 34-99418) and author a specific analysis of how each key rule-change reshapes the specific-de-SPAC-transaction choreography, specific sponsor economics, specific target-company liability exposure, and specific-de-SPAC-vs-traditional-IPO decision. The deliverable is a counsel-and-board-ready analysis that could inform a *specific* decision — whether a specific target would still pursue de-SPAC, under what specific conditions, with what specific protective structuring. By the end of the exercise you should be able to explain the specific pre-2024 vs. post-2024 regime deltas to a board that previously defaulted to de-SPAC and now needs to reconsider.

## Background

This exercise covers material from:

- [Chapter 7 — IPO Alternatives — Direct Listing, SPAC / de-SPAC, DPO, and Reg A+](../07-ipo-alternatives.md)

## Starter guidance

Three anti-patterns to avoid:

- **The "secondary-summary read" shortcut.** The 2024 rules are a specific SEC release, and the specific-text matters. Secondary summaries (practitioner-blog posts, newsletter articles) are useful orientation but are not substitutes for the specific-text read. Open the specific rule-release and read it.
- **The "pre-2024 de-SPAC data" misapplication.** Historical de-SPAC data (2020-2023) reflects a materially different liability-and-economics regime. Specific-data-points like "de-SPAC transactions typically close in X months" or "specific-aggressive-projections produced specific-post-close-performance patterns" need to be read in the specific-pre-2024 context and may not apply post-2024.
- **The "de-SPAC is dead" overstatement.** De-SPAC remains a specific-option under the post-2024 rules for specific-fit targets. The specific-exercise is to identify the specific-conditions under which it fits, not to conclude it is categorically eliminated.
