# exercise-03: PCAOB Auditor Selection and 2-Year Audit Plan

**Estimated effort:** 4 hours

## Objective

Take the frozen pre-IPO company profile you carried out of exercise-01 and produce a defensible auditor-engagement package — a firm-selection memo, a Reg S-X Rule 2-01 independence workbook, a reissuance-vs.-reaudit workplan for each historical fiscal year presented in the S-1, an audit-committee choreography brief, an interim-review and comfort-letter calendar, an Item 304 auditor-change disclosure kit, and a sector-specific audit-complexity plan. By the end you should be able to walk the package into an audit-committee meeting and defend the specific firm recommendation, the specific pre-existing engagements to unwind, the specific reaudit calendar, and the specific CAM and preferability-letter posture against a critical audit-committee chair, a critical PCAOB-inspection-savvy partner candidate, and a critical IPO-counsel partner.

> Reminder: this module is education, not legal / accounting / tax advice. Auditor engagement is negotiated by qualified counsel, cleared by the audit committee under a documented pre-approval regime, and executed under the standards in force on the specific engagement dates.

## Background

This exercise covers material from:

- [Chapter 3 — PCAOB Auditor Engagement and the 2-Year Audit History](../03-pcaob-auditor-engagement-and-audit-history.md)

Chapter 1's readiness framing and chapter 2's S-1 authoring workstream are assumed. Chapter 4's SOX 404 programme, chapter 5's EGC-election framework, chapter 6's dual-track structure, chapter 7's listing-standard graduation, and chapter 8's EDGAR choreography are downstream — you do not need them here. The point of this exercise is to install the specific practitioner discipline of running auditor selection and the 2-year (or 3-year) audit history as a documented workstream against the specific SEC and PCAOB rules rather than as a founder-CFO "we like our current firm" default.

## Prerequisites

- The frozen pre-IPO company profile from exercise-01. Do not re-open the profile; carry it forward unchanged.
- Current auditor firm and PCAOB registration status (registered / not registered; if registered, office and lead-partner name).
- Most recent audit report opinion type (unmodified / unmodified with emphasis-of-matter / qualified / adverse / disclaimer) and the specific audit-standard framework used (AICPA SAS / PCAOB AS).
- Whether any prior audit-firm change has occurred in the last 24 months; if so, the effective date and the specific reason.
- Current controller and CAO backgrounds — prior firms, tenure, whether either was on the current or a prior audit team (relevant to the Rule 2-01(c)(2)(iii) FROR cooling-off).
- List of pre-IPO options / RSUs granted in the past 24 months with grant dates, exercise / strike prices, and the specific 409A valuation date each grant priced against.
- List of related-party transactions above the $120,000 Item 404 threshold (party, amount, business purpose, board / audit-committee approval status).
- Access to SEC EDGAR, the PCAOB inspection-reports database, the PCAOB firm-registration search, and the sector's comparable-issuer S-1 filings identified in exercise-01.

## Tasks

### 1. Firm-tier selection scorecard and recommendation

Score **3–5 candidate firms** — a mix of Big Four (PwC, Deloitte, EY, KPMG) and next-tier (Grant Thornton, BDO USA, RSM US, Baker Tilly, Crowe LLP). For each candidate, produce:

- **PCAOB registration status** — Sarbanes-Oxley Section 102 confirmation from the PCAOB registration search, including the specific offices where the engagement team would sit.
- **Sector bench** — the specific comparable-issuer engagements the firm holds in the sub-sector (source from the S-1 auditor-consent exhibits of the comp set identified in exercise-01).
- **Engagement-partner track record** — the specific proposed lead partner, prior IPO engagements, prior tenure on the client's sub-sector, and any inspection history against that partner.
- **Sarbanes-Oxley Section 104 inspection posture** — the specific Part I.A and Part I.B findings from the most-recent PCAOB inspection report on the firm and, where visible, on the specific proposed engagement partner and comparable engagements. Note any confidential Part II remediation posture inferable from public statements or the 12-month remediation window.
- **Fee estimate** — recurring annual audit fee, IPO-year incremental fee, and reaudit fee per historical fiscal year (use general practitioner terms; do not invent statistics).
- **Comparable-issuer signalling to the buy-side pricing meeting** — how the firm reads against the sub-sector's public-cohort auditor mix (chapter 1 pricing-meeting frame).

Conclude with a **specific selection recommendation** with a written rationale that a critical audit-committee chair can accept or specifically disagree with. If the recommendation is a switch from the current auditor, sequence the Item 304 disclosure planning in Task 7.

### 2. Reg S-X Rule 2-01 independence analysis

Run the recommended firm from Task 1 through the full **Rule 2-01** analysis. Produce a workbook that walks each subsection:

- **Rule 2-01(c)(4) — non-audit-service prohibitions.** For each category — bookkeeping / accounting records; financial-information-systems design and implementation; appraisal / valuation / fairness opinions; actuarial; internal-audit outsourcing; management functions or HR; broker-dealer / investment-adviser / investment-banking; legal; expert services unrelated to the audit — identify any specific pre-existing engagement between the firm and the company (or between the firm and any FRO-role individual, in the case of personal tax) that must be unwound before the engagement letter is signed. Include tax-planning, IT-consulting, and 409A-valuation engagements specifically.
- **Rule 2-01(c)(3) — business relationships.** Identify any direct business relationship (customer, joint-venture partner, co-investor) between the firm and the company outside ordinary-course consumer capacity.
- **Rule 2-01(c)(2) — employment relationships,** including the **Rule 2-01(c)(2)(iii)** 1-year cooling-off for former audit-team members joining the client in a **Financial-Reporting-Oversight Role** (CFO, controller, CAO, or any role reviewing / supervising financial reporting). Cross-check against the current controller and CAO backgrounds from the prerequisites.
- **Rule 2-01(c)(6) — partner rotation.** 5-year lead partner with 5-year time-out; 5-year concurring (EQR) partner with 3-year time-out; 7-year other audit partners with 2-year time-out. Confirm the proposed lead partner will still be within the 5-year window through the IPO and the two following public audit cycles.

Produce a **specific list of engagements to unwind or restructure before the engagement letter**, with owners and target dates.

### 3. Reissuance vs. reaudit workplan per historical fiscal year

For **each historical fiscal year** that will be presented in the S-1 (2 years for an EGC under JOBS Act Section 7(a)(2)(A); 3 income-statement years / 2 balance-sheet years for a non-EGC under Reg S-X Rules 3-01 and 3-02), produce a specific decision and workplan.

Where the historical firm **is PCAOB-registered** and will cooperate:

- Draft the **reissuance workstream** — workpaper re-read, additional PCAOB-specific procedures the AICPA engagement did not require, updated subsequent-events review through the S-1 filing date, and the re-issued opinion.
- Sequence the **AS 6101** predecessor-auditor communication with the incoming firm (whether or not the historical firm remains as re-issuer).

Where the historical firm **is not PCAOB-registered, will not re-issue, or where workpapers do not support reissuance**:

- Draft the **reaudit calendar** — 3–9 months typical, per period; identify the specific calendar-critical path against the target confidential-DRS submission date.
- Estimate the **reaudit fee** — 2–3x annual audit fee per reaudited fiscal year, in general practitioner terms.
- Plan the substantive-testing scope, third-party confirmations, physical-inventory-observation-equivalent procedures for closed periods, and full workpaper documentation.
- Sequence the **AS 6101** predecessor-auditor communication and the consent-gathering for the S-1 auditor-consent exhibit.

Conclude with a **specific per-year decision** (reissuance / reaudit) and a **combined calendar** that lines up against the target S-1 confidential-DRS date.

### 4. Audit-committee choreography

Draft the specific audit-committee choreography for the engagement:

- **Sarbanes-Oxley Section 202 pre-approval regime** — the specific split between specifically-pre-approved services (each engagement itemised and approved) and generally-pre-approved categories (audit-related, tax-compliance, tax-planning; each with a fee ceiling). Include the **de minimis exception** parameters. Codify in the audit-committee charter and each meeting's pre-approval schedule.
- **PCAOB Rule 3526 annual independence-confirmation cadence** — engagement-start letter, annual refresh, and each meeting's private-session review.
- **AS 1301 required communications** at engagement start, planning-phase before fieldwork, and completion before report issuance — audit scope, significant accounting policies, significant unusual transactions, critical accounting estimates, disagreements with management, ICFR deficiencies, uncorrected misstatements, and other AS 1301 topics.
- **Private-session cadence with management excluded** — every regular audit-committee meeting; frequency of ad-hoc sessions; the specific standing agenda for the private session (management cooperation, tone-at-the-top on accounting judgement, previously unraised concerns).
- **Engagement-letter and fee-approval mechanics** — who negotiates (audit-committee chair with CFO), who signs, and how the fee-schedule amendments during the IPO year are pre-approved.
- **Minute-taking discipline** — the specific level of detail that supports the SEC / PCAOB / litigation record; who drafts, who reviews, when minutes are approved.

### 5. AS 4105 interim-review calendar

Build the interim-review calendar under **AS 4105** (historically **AU 722 / SAS 100**) covering:

- **Each stub-period** included in the S-1 at initial confidential-DRS submission and at each amendment through effectiveness.
- **Each post-IPO 10-Q** for the first four quarters after effectiveness.

For each review, specify the negative-assurance mechanics (inquiries and analytical procedures rather than substantive audit testing), the written communication to the audit committee, and the filing-blocker discipline (no S-1 amendment or 10-Q filing until the review is complete). Line the review calendar up against the audit-committee meeting calendar from Task 4.

### 6. AU 634 / SAS 72 comfort-letter circle-up workflow

Draft the comfort-letter workflow under **AU 634 / SAS 72**:

- **Circle-up mechanics** — underwriter-counsel-driven marking of every number in the prospectus draft the underwriters want covered; transmission to the auditor; auditor coverage negotiation, pushback where a specific representation cannot be given, and alternative-coverage proposals. Iterate over the 2–4 weeks before pricing.
- **Pricing-date comfort letter** — delivered to the underwriters at pricing.
- **Bring-down comfort letter** — delivered at closing, typically **T+3 business days** after pricing.
- **Written representation categories** the letter covers — auditor independence; that the audited financials comply as to form with the Securities Act and Reg S-X; negative assurance on specified unaudited data (interim data, subsequent-events data, capitalisation tables, selected-financial-data reconciliations, ratios computed from audited numbers); tick-and-tie on the specific circled figures.

Identify the specific working-group owners (underwriter counsel, issuer counsel, auditor, CFO / controller) and the specific hand-off milestones.

### 7. Item 304 auditor-change disclosure kit

If the recommendation in Task 1 involves a switch inside the 2-year lookback, draft the **Item 304 of Reg S-K** disclosure elements for the S-1:

- Identity of the former auditor.
- Whether the change is a dismissal, resignation, or declination to stand for re-appointment; the specific effective date.
- Opinion type on each of the past two years' reports (unmodified / modified / qualified / adverse / disclaimer; emphasis-of-matter language).
- Whether the audit committee or the board recommended or approved the change (minute reference).
- Whether any **disagreements** on accounting principles, financial-statement disclosure, or auditing scope occurred; whether any **Item 304(a)(1)(v) reportable events** occurred (material-weakness or reliability-of-representations findings, unwillingness to rely on management's representations, expanded-scope requests, restatement events).
- Plan the **Item 601(b)(16)** former-auditor letter mechanics — request cadence, former-auditor 10-business-day window to respond, filing as an S-1 exhibit.
- Note the parallel **Form 8-K Item 4.01 4-business-day** cadence for auditor changes post-IPO.

### 8. Sector-specific audit-complexity plan

For the company's sector (from the frozen exercise-01 profile), identify the specific technical-accounting areas that will drive audit workload and CAM candidacy:

- **ASC 606 revenue recognition** — performance-obligation identification (subscription, professional services, implementation, training, hardware, resold third-party components); transaction-price allocation; variable consideration (usage-based, tiered, discounts, rebates, SLA credits); principal-vs.-agent for reseller and channel arrangements; contract-modification accounting for ramp deals.
- **ASC 718 stock-based compensation** — Black-Scholes / Monte Carlo / lattice model selection; expected-volatility, term, risk-free-rate, and dividend-yield inputs; the pre-IPO 409A valuation cadence from the prerequisites; **cheap-stock exposure** from grants at values materially below the eventual IPO range; the retrospective valuation-adjustment risk the auditor and SEC staff will test.
- **ASC 842 leases** — right-of-use assets and lease liabilities, discount-rate estimation, portfolio-level disclosure.
- **ASC 740 income taxes** — deferred-tax-asset **valuation-allowance** analysis under the "more likely than not" standard; likely full valuation allowance pre-profitability; the eventual release event as a specific S-1 / 10-K disclosure and consultation matter.
- **ASC 805 business combinations and pushdown** — if the company was previously part of a parent (PE holdco, corporate parent spinout), the **push-down vs. carve-out** presentation decision and its effect on reported earnings.

Produce a **one-page-per-topic technical-accounting brief** the auditor's national office will actually receive.

### 9. AS 3101 Critical Audit Matters plan

Plan the CAMs posture under **AS 3101**:

- Confirm the **EGC exemption** where applicable (EGCs are exempt from CAMs disclosure for the duration of EGC status).
- Where the company is **non-EGC** or plans for **post-EGC** cycles, identify the specific accounts / disclosures likely to surface as CAMs (revenue recognition on a specific contract type; deferred-tax-asset valuation-allowance analysis; goodwill or intangible-asset impairment; stock-based-compensation cheap-stock; specific revenue-cutoff areas).
- Plan the **drafting negotiation** with the engagement partner — the pre-filing weeks in which auditor and CFO / controller iterate on CAM language, the audit-committee review, and the interaction with the risk-factor and MD&A drafts.

### 10. Item 601(b)(18) preferability-letter planning

Identify any voluntary accounting-principle changes contemplated in the S-1 or the first-year 10-K that would trigger a **preferability letter** under **Item 601(b)(18) of Reg S-K** — a revenue-recognition method not compelled by a new standard, FIFO / LIFO switch, depreciation-method change, or similar. Plan the auditor consultation path (national-office involvement typical) and the exhibit filing.

## Starter guidance

Anti-patterns to avoid:

- **The "our private-company auditor will re-issue" default.** The historical firm's PCAOB registration and willingness to re-issue are two separate questions. A firm that is registered but has not staffed a PCAOB-standard reissuance in the sector on the calendar you need is a filing-blocker dressed as a co-operative predecessor. The specific test is a written reissuance-scope letter and a named engagement partner with a specific calendar, not a friendly phone call.
- **The pre-approval regime as a rubber-stamp.** A generally-pre-approved-categories schedule with no fee ceilings and no de-minimis discipline is not a Section 202 regime — it is a paper trail waiting for a comment letter. The specific practitioner discipline is a schedule that a critical outside reader (or an SEC staff reviewer) reads and sees the audit committee actually making the pre-approval call.
- **The "we're EGC, so CAMs don't matter" frame.** EGC exemption from AS 3101 CAMs disclosure is real, but the accounts that would be CAMs post-EGC are the same accounts the auditor is scrutinising during EGC status and the same accounts the SEC staff will comment on. Skipping the CAM analysis during EGC status defers a workstream you will need on the first non-EGC audit anyway.
- **The reaudit-fee sticker shock deferral.** A reaudit that lands 4 months before the target confidential-DRS date is the auditor's leverage on every other engagement term. The specific discipline is to price the reaudit into the engagement letter early, sequence the AS 6101 predecessor communication, and remove the calendar surprise before it becomes an economic one.

## Acceptance criteria

You can demonstrate that:

- The firm-selection scorecard covers 3–5 named candidates with a specific PCAOB registration confirmation, a specific inspection-history read against the proposed lead partner, and a specific recommendation with rationale.
- The Rule 2-01 workbook walks each subsection ((c)(2), (c)(2)(iii), (c)(3), (c)(4), (c)(6)) and identifies specific pre-existing engagements to unwind before the engagement letter.
- The reissuance-vs.-reaudit workplan produces a specific per-year decision and a combined calendar that lines up against the target confidential-DRS date.
- The audit-committee choreography brief covers Section 202 pre-approval (with fee ceilings and de-minimis mechanics), Rule 3526 cadence, AS 1301 communications, private-session cadence, engagement-letter mechanics, and minute discipline.
- The AS 4105 interim-review calendar covers every S-1 stub-period and the first four post-IPO 10-Q reviews with the filing-blocker gating identified.
- The AU 634 / SAS 72 comfort-letter workflow covers circle-up, pricing-date and T+3 bring-down letters, and the specific written-representation categories.
- The Item 304 disclosure kit produces the specific drafting elements and the Item 601(b)(16) mechanics; the Form 8-K Item 4.01 4-business-day cadence is noted for post-IPO.
- The sector-specific audit-complexity plan produces a one-page-per-topic brief covering ASC 606, ASC 718 (with the specific 409A cheap-stock exposure), ASC 842, ASC 740, and any ASC 805 pushdown / carve-out issues.
- The AS 3101 CAM plan resolves the EGC-exemption question and identifies specific CAM candidates for post-EGC cycles.
- The Item 601(b)(18) preferability-letter analysis identifies any voluntary accounting-principle change and the auditor consultation path.
- A critical outside reader (an audit-committee chair, an IPO-counsel partner, a PCAOB-inspection-savvy engagement partner) could challenge each recommendation and see the specific evidence behind it.

## Stretch goals

- **Alternate-firm scenario.** Redo Task 1 assuming the top-ranked firm has an independence conflict discovered after the engagement letter is drafted. Which specific next candidate steps into the seat, and what specific calendar and fee re-negotiation results?
- **Post-IPO auditor-change dry-run.** Draft the specific **Form 8-K Item 4.01** filing and the accompanying **Item 601(b)(16)** former-auditor letter for a hypothetical post-IPO auditor change 18 months after effectiveness — including the specific 4-business-day cadence, the audit-committee approval documentation, and the disagreement / reportable-event analysis under Item 304.
- **Comparable-issuer CAM benchmark.** Pull the AS 3101 CAM sections from the first three post-EGC 10-Ks of the sub-sector comp set identified in exercise-01. What specific patterns emerge in the accounts / disclosures the auditors flag as CAMs? Which of those CAMs would you expect to surface in the company's own first non-EGC audit, and what specific pre-filing workstream would you build now to shape the language?
- **Sector-specific 409A cheap-stock deep-dive.** For the pre-IPO options / RSUs granted in the past 24 months from the prerequisites, model the retrospective valuation-adjustment scenario assuming the eventual IPO range is 2x, 3x, and 5x the most recent 409A value. What specific ASC 718 remeasurement and additional-stock-compensation-expense results? What specific SEC comment-letter posture would you expect?
