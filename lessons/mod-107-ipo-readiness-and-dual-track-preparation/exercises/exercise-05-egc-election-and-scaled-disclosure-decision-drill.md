# exercise-05: EGC Election and Scaled-Disclosure Decision Drill

**Estimated effort:** 3 hours

## Objective

Take the frozen pre-IPO company profile you carried in from exercise-01 and produce a defensible EGC-election deliverable — a CFO-authored memo to the audit committee that (1) confirms Section 2(a)(19) qualification, (2) models each of the four early-termination triggers against the current forecast, (3) makes an "elect vs. leave" recommendation on every scaled-disclosure accommodation with an explicit signalling-analysis paragraph, (4) documents the irrevocable Section 107(b) new-standard-adoption election, (5) sequences the EGC-exit workstream against the earliest projected termination trigger, (6) drafts the specific S-1 disclosure language that memorialises the elections, and (7) runs the audit-committee-approval choreography. By the end you should be able to walk the memo into an audit-committee meeting and defend each election against a critical audit-committee chair, a critical outside counsel, and a critical PCAOB-registered auditor.

> Reminder: this module is education, not legal / accounting / tax advice. Every specific EGC election is authored and cleared by qualified securities counsel and coordinated with the PCAOB-registered auditor. The Section 107(b) accounting-standard-adoption election is *irrevocable* once made in the first S-1 — the audit committee approves it with that irrevocability squarely in view.

## Background

This exercise covers material from:

- [Chapter 5 — JOBS Act EGC Election and Scaled Disclosure](../05-jobs-act-egc-election-and-scaled-disclosure.md)

The EGC election reshapes several other workstreams you have already installed. The 2-vs.-3 audited-year decision under Section 7(a)(2)(A) interlocks with the reaudit / PCAOB engagement work from [chapter 3](../03-pcaob-auditor-engagement-and-audit-history.md); the SOX 404(b) auditor-attestation deferral interlocks with the ICFR programme design from [chapter 4](../04-sox-404-icfr-programme-design.md); the cover-page and "Implications" disclosure language interlocks with the S-1 authoring workflow from [chapter 2](../02-s-1-registration-statement-authoring.md). Refer at the boundary; this exercise does not re-teach the S-1 drafting mechanics, the reaudit programme, or the ICFR mechanics — it installs the EGC overlay that reshapes each of them.

The point of the exercise is to install the specific discipline of running the elect / decline decision as a documented, audit-committee-approved workstream against specific facts and specific signalling considerations rather than as an "elect everything the statute offers" default.

- Projected non-affiliate public float at each second-fiscal-quarter measurement date over the next 5 years (management plan, using the same share-price assumption used elsewhere in the readiness memo): Q2 FY+1 $_____; Q2 FY+2 $_____; Q2 FY+3 $_____; Q2 FY+4 $_____; Q2 FY+5 $_____

- Current adoption status of ASC 842 (leases): _____; current adoption status of ASC 326 (CECL): _____

If any field does not apply (e.g., no non-convertible debt planned), note *why* rather than skipping.

## Tasks

### 1. EGC qualification analysis under Section 2(a)(19)

Apply the specific Section 2(a)(19) two-part test to the frozen profile.

- **Revenue test.** Compare most-recently-completed-fiscal-year total annual gross revenues against the inflation-adjusted threshold (currently ~$1.235B, *as most recently adjusted by SEC release — verify the current amount against the most recent SEC release before relying*). Cite the specific SEC release you verified against. Confirm the revenue measure is top-line under US GAAP (or IFRS-as-issued-by-the-IASB for an FPI), not a management-adjusted figure.
- **First-sale-of-common-equity timing test.** Confirm the issuer's first sale of common equity securities pursuant to an effective registration statement is *after* December 8, 2011 — for a first-time IPO this is trivially satisfied at the point the S-1 is declared effective; document the confirmation.
- **FPI mapping (if applicable).** If the issuer will file on Form F-1 and report on Form 20-F, walk the FPI-eligibility test and confirm the EGC accommodations flow through to the FPI regime. Note the specific F-1 / 20-F disclosure architecture is different from the S-1 / 10-K pair — refer at the boundary; do not re-teach.

Conclude with a specific yes / no on EGC qualification. Attach the working paper (revenue figure, threshold, release citation, timing confirmation).

### 2. Early-termination trigger model under Section 2(a)(19)(A)–(D)

Model the projected termination date under each of the four Section 2(a)(19) triggers against the frozen 5-year plan. Produce a specific date (or a specific fiscal-year-end) for each trigger.

- **Trigger (A) — revenue.** Last day of the fiscal year in which total annual gross revenues equal or exceed the inflation-adjusted threshold (~$1.235B — verify current amount). Walk the 5-year revenue projection year-by-year; identify the fiscal year the threshold is crossed (or note "not crossed within 5 years").
- **Trigger (B) — five-year clock.** Last day of the fiscal year following the fifth anniversary of the first sale of common equity securities pursuant to an effective registration statement. Compute against the assumed effectiveness date and fiscal-year-end from the frozen profile.
- **Trigger (C) — $1B non-convertible debt.** Date on which the issuer has issued more than $1B in non-convertible debt securities during the previous three-year period. Walk the planned issuance schedule; identify the crossing date (or note "not crossed within the modelled horizon"). Convertible debt does not count against the threshold; confirm each instrument's classification. <!-- needs-research: confirm any SEC interpretive guidance on the $1B non-convertible debt calculation including whether refinancings and 144A private placements count -->
- **Trigger (D) — large accelerated filer.** Date the issuer becomes a large accelerated filer under Rule 12b-2 of the Exchange Act — generally aggregate worldwide market value of common equity held by non-affiliates (public float) of $700M or more measured at the last business day of the most recently completed second fiscal quarter, *plus* subject-to-Section-13(a)-or-15(d) reporting for at least 12 months, *plus* at least one annual report filed. Walk the 5-year Q2 public-float projection year-by-year; identify the specific Q2 measurement at which the $700M threshold is crossed *and* the 12-month-plus-one-annual-report prerequisite is satisfied.

Identify the **earliest-projected termination date** across the four triggers. Rebase all downstream planning (Task 5) to that date rather than the five-year outer bound.

### 3. Scaled-disclosure elect-vs.-leave decision matrix

Produce a specific elect / decline recommendation on each accommodation below. For each, output: (a) the specific statutory or regulatory citation, (b) the cost / workload benefit of electing, (c) the specific comparability signalling impact, (d) a recommendation, and (e) a **signalling-analysis paragraph** that explicitly weighs the CFO's "elect-all vs. elect-selectively" posture against your specific buy-side book (institutional cross-holders, ISS / Glass Lewis stewardship posture, sector-comparable disclosure practice).

- **Two vs. three years of audited financials under Section 7(a)(2)(A) of the Securities Act.** Interlocks with the chapter-3 reaudit / PCAOB engagement plan (refer at the boundary; do not re-teach). Address reaudit-work saved and the comparability signalling implication of a two-year set relative to non-EGC sector comparables.
- **Reduced Item 402 executive-compensation disclosure.** Cover each sub-election separately: CEO + 2 NEOs vs. CEO + 4 NEOs; omit CD&A vs. include; exemption from pay-versus-performance under Item 402(v) of Reg S-K (implementing Dodd-Frank Section 953(a)) vs. voluntary compliance; exemption from pay-ratio under Item 402(u) implementing Dodd-Frank Section 953(b) vs. voluntary compliance; exemption from say-on-pay under Exchange Act Section 14A vs. voluntary annual vote.
- **Deferred SOX 404(b) auditor ICFR attestation.** Confirm the deferral election. Note that Section 404(a) management-assessment applicability still turns on with the second annual report — refer at the boundary to chapter 4; do not re-teach the 404(a) / 404(b) split.
- **Deferred Critical Audit Matter (CAM) disclosure under AS 3101.** Confirm the deferral election and its interaction with the auditor's report format in the S-1 and in the first-year 10-K.
- **Confidential DRS submission under Section 6(e) of the Securities Act.** Confirm the confidential submission election (now standard across essentially all issuers post the SEC's 2017 policy expansion) and specify the anticipated public-flip timing relative to the 15-day-before-roadshow rule under Section 6(e)(1).
- **Testing-the-waters under Section 5(d) of the Securities Act** with QIBs (Rule 144A) and institutional accredited investors (Rule 501(a)). Note that SEC Rule 163B (2019) extended the TTW privilege to all issuers, not just EGCs; the specific TTW mechanics live in mod-108 chapter 2 — refer at the boundary; do not re-teach. Confirm the election and paper-trail discipline at high level.
- **FAST Act (2015) accommodation** to omit from the initial DRS submission (and subsequent amendments and initial public filing) any interim financial statements the issuer reasonably believes will not be required at effectiveness. Walk the specific quarter-boundary calendar for the frozen filing plan; confirm election and the "reasonably believes" documentation.

The signalling-analysis paragraph for each item is the load-bearing artefact — it forces the CFO to author, not just check a box.

### 4. Section 107(b) irrevocable accounting-standard-adoption election

Decide **once, at first S-1 filing**, whether to opt into private-company / non-PBE transition dates or public-company / PBE transition dates for FASB Accounting Standards Updates. The election is irrevocable — document the irrevocability throughout.

- Score the specific transition schedule for ASC 842 (leases) against the company's specific facts (right-of-use assets and lease-liability impact, real-estate footprint, equipment leases; current adoption status from the frozen profile).
- Score the specific transition schedule for ASC 326 (CECL) against the company's specific facts (financial-instrument exposure, trade-receivable expected-loss modelling; current adoption status).
- Score each pending FASB ASU with a PBE / non-PBE effective-date split (from the frozen prerequisite list). For each, identify the deferral window, the P&L / balance-sheet impact of adopting on the earlier vs. later date, and the auditor's input.
- Document the auditor's input on each standard (this is a first-S-1-drafting conversation with the PCAOB-registered auditor; capture the auditor's position on comparability, transition-method preference, and the cost of parallel-tracking vs. single-track adoption).
- Produce a single specific election: **opt in to deferral** (apply new standards on the non-PBE date, retain optionality to voluntarily accelerate any specific standard without losing the deferral for others) **or opt out of deferral** (apply new standards on the PBE date; election is irrevocable).
- Document the audit-committee approval, the specific committee-resolution language, and a plain-English statement of the *irrevocability* — the memo the audit committee approves should not leave any ambiguity that opting in cannot be reversed and opting out cannot be reversed.

### 5. EGC-exit workstream sequencing

Keying to the **earliest-projected termination trigger from Task 2**, build the workstream calendar to have every deferred obligation operational at exit. For each workstream produce: (a) specific milestones, (b) owners (CFO / controller / GC / VP-IR / audit committee / auditor / comp consultant / IR counsel), and (c) target dates rebased to the earliest trigger.

- The **SOX 404(b) programme** — control documentation, testing infrastructure, deficiency-remediation cadence, auditor shadow-audit dry-run, first-year full-scope 404(b) attestation ready at termination. Interlocks with chapter 4 — refer at the boundary; do not re-teach ICFR mechanics.
- **Full Item 402 disclosure**, including CD&A drafting workstream, compensation-consultant engagement and independence review under exchange listing-standard consultant-independence rules, peer-group construction, and rehearsal in a mock proxy.
- The **first pay-versus-performance disclosure under Item 402(v)** — the required table, the required footnote reconciliations, and the required company-selected-measure narrative.
- The **first pay-ratio disclosure under Item 402(u)** implementing Dodd-Frank Section 953(b) — the median-employee identification methodology, the CEO-comp calculation, the ratio, and the narrative.
- The **first say-on-pay proxy design** — the required frequency vote (say-on-frequency) at the same meeting, the shareholder-engagement plan, the ISS / Glass Lewis pre-read.
- The **first CAM negotiation** with the auditor under AS 3101 — the auditor's identification of Critical Audit Matters, the CFO / audit-committee review process, and the drafting choreography that lands in the first-year auditor's report post-termination.
- The **full 3-year audit history** — if the second-year election was taken under Section 7(a)(2)(A), the reaudit / build workstream that adds the third year at the first post-EGC 10-K. Interlocks with chapter 3 — refer at the boundary; do not re-teach the reaudit programme.

Produce a specific Gantt or milestone table with owner, target date, and dependency. Flag the earliest-trigger date on the chart so the reader sees the delta between plan and deadline.

### 6. Draft S-1 disclosure language

Draft the specific disclosure language that memorialises the elections in the S-1. Each section should be authored to the level a working-group counsel would review, not to a placeholder-sentence level.

- **Cover-page EGC identification.** The specific cover-page box / statement identifying the issuer as an emerging growth company under the JOBS Act.
- **"Implications of Being an Emerging Growth Company" section** in the front of the prospectus (typically in the summary of the offering). Enumerate each elected accommodation with a specific statutory reference and a specific plain-English description of the accommodation and its effect on disclosure.
- **Risk factor addressing reduced disclosure and comparability.** Draft the specific risk factor addressing (a) the reduced-disclosure regime relative to non-EGC issuers, (b) the comparability impact on investors accustomed to non-EGC disclosure, and (c) the specific accommodations elected (list them with statutory citations).
- **Section 107(b) irrevocability statement.** Draft the specific statement disclosing the Section 107(b) election (opt-in or opt-out) *and* the specific plain-English statement that the election is irrevocable. This is the single most-important disclosure to get right because a reader reversing the elected posture later has no recourse.

### 7. Audit-committee approval choreography

Author the specific choreography that carries the elections through the audit committee.

- **Pre-meeting CFO memo to the audit committee.** The memo should present each election (Tasks 3 and 4), the specific recommendation, the cost / comparability trade-off, and — for the Section 107(b) election — a specific highlighted section on the *irrevocability* of the decision. Circulate at least 5 business days before the meeting.
- **Committee resolution.** Draft specific resolution language approving (a) each Task-3 scaled-disclosure election, (b) the Task-4 Section 107(b) election with an explicit irrevocability acknowledgment, and (c) the delegation to the CFO and outside counsel to reflect the elections in the S-1 disclosure and in the auditor engagement letter.
- **Board ratification if required.** Note whether the audit-committee charter (or the applicable state-law / exchange-rule framework) requires full-board ratification of any of the elections. If so, draft the board-resolution language.
- **Coordination with auditor and outside counsel.** Specify the sequencing — auditor sign-off on the Section 107(b) election (auditor's position on comparability and transition-method risk); outside counsel sign-off on the disclosure language (Task 6); underwriter counsel input on the "Implications" language given comparable-issuer S-1 precedent.
- **Minute-taking discipline.** The Section 107(b) election is documented with specific attention to irrevocability — the minutes reflect the committee's specific acknowledgment that the election cannot be reversed. This matters because a later stewardship inquiry (from an activist, from ISS, from an SEC comment letter re-opening an accounting policy footnote) will look to the audit-committee minutes as the record of the decision.

## Starter guidance

Three anti-patterns to avoid:

- **The "elect everything the statute offers" default.** Electing every accommodation without an author-level signalling analysis is administratively easy and lands as "we took every relief" to a comparability-sensitive buy-side book. The specific discipline is to produce a specific signalling paragraph per election, not to check every box.
- **The Section 107(b) drift.** Treating the Section 107(b) election as a routine footnote-language question rather than an irrevocable one-time decision reviewed at the audit-committee level with the auditor in the room. A CFO who realises in year 3 that the "opt in to deferral" decision has created an unwanted comparability gap with a non-EGC peer set — and who then discovers the election cannot be reversed — is running a signalling problem the statute made permanent.
- **The five-year-outer-bound plan.** Sequencing the 404(b) build, the Item 402 CD&A build, and the say-on-pay design against the five-year default trigger, when the frozen forecast projects a large-accelerated-filer trigger at year 3 Q2. A CFO who plans to the outer bound and hits an earlier trigger is running an ICFR audit and a full-Item-402 proxy build in flight — the specific worst-case scenario the exercise is designed to prevent.

## Acceptance criteria

You can demonstrate that:

- The EGC qualification analysis in Task 1 produces a specific yes / no conclusion with a specific SEC-release citation for the current inflation-adjusted revenue threshold.
- The Task 2 termination-trigger model produces a specific projected date (or fiscal-year-end) for each of Section 2(a)(19)(A) through (D) and identifies the earliest.
- The Task 3 decision matrix carries an explicit elect / decline recommendation for every listed accommodation, each with a signalling-analysis paragraph the reader can either accept or specifically disagree with.
- The Task 4 Section 107(b) memo produces a single specific election, documents auditor input on each of ASC 842, ASC 326, and each pending PBE-vs.-non-PBE ASU, and reflects the irrevocability in the audit-committee resolution and minutes.
- The Task 5 workstream calendar is rebased to the earliest-projected termination trigger from Task 2, not the five-year outer bound, and assigns specific owners and specific target dates.
- The Task 6 disclosure drafts (cover-page, "Implications" section, risk factor, irrevocability statement) are drafted at working-group-counsel-review level, not placeholder level.
- The Task 7 audit-committee choreography produces a pre-meeting memo, a committee resolution (with irrevocability acknowledgment for Section 107(b)), a coordination plan with auditor and outside counsel, and a minute-taking discipline specific enough that a subsequent stewardship inquiry can trace the decision.
- A critical outside reader (audit-committee chair, IPO counsel partner, PCAOB-registered engagement partner) could challenge each election and see the specific evidence the memo relies on.
