# Resources for mod-107-ipo-readiness-and-dual-track-preparation

Primary source documentation, practitioner canon, data providers, and legal / accounting references used by this module. Prefer these over secondary write-ups: they are authoritative, and — for the disclosure and audit sources especially — these are the sources the SEC staff, the auditors, and the underwriter working group will actually cite in a comment letter or a working-group call.

## Primary SEC materials

- **SEC EDGAR** — the source of truth for confidential DRS submissions, public S-1 filings, amendments, comment letters (staff comment letters and issuer responses are typically posted 20+ business days after the offering closes), 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K / DEF 14A / Form 4 filings, and every downstream disclosure. Search by company or by filing type. <https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search-and-access>
- **SEC EDGAR Filer Manual** — the specific submission-mechanics reference used by issuer counsel and financial printers. <https://www.sec.gov/edgar/filer/filer-manuals-standards>
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance — Financial Reporting Manual (FRM)** — the specific staff reference used to answer accounting-disclosure questions in the S-1 review; updated regularly and cited on staff calls. <https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual>
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance — Compliance and Disclosure Interpretations (C&DIs)** — the specific staff Q&A guidance across Reg S-K, Reg S-X, Securities Act Rules, Exchange Act Rules; treated as authoritative by practitioner counsel. <https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual>
- **Regulation S-K (17 CFR 229)** — the specific non-financial disclosure requirements for S-1s and periodic reports (Items 101 business, 105 risk factors, 202 capital stock, 303 MD&A, 304 changes in accountants, 401 directors/officers, 402 executive comp, 403 principal holders, 404 related-party transactions, 407 governance, 601 exhibits, 512 undertakings, and the 2023 additions at 106 cybersecurity and 402(w) clawback). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229>
- **Regulation S-X (17 CFR 210)** — the specific financial-statement form and content requirements (Article 1 general; Article 2 auditors and independence; Article 3 general instructions; Article 5 commercial and industrial companies; Article 8 smaller reporting company; Article 10 interim; Article 11 pro forma). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-210>
- **Regulation FD (17 CFR 243)** — the fair-disclosure prohibition on selective disclosure of material non-public information. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-243>
- **Regulation G (17 CFR 244)** — non-GAAP financial-measure reconciliation requirements. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-244>
- **Regulation S-T (17 CFR 232)** — EDGAR filing rules, including Rule 405 inline-XBRL tagging requirements. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-232>
- **Regulation M-A (17 CFR 229.1000)** — tender-offer and M&A disclosure obligations; relevant in dual-track deals where a tender or exchange offer converges with an S-1 process. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229>

## The JOBS Act and EGC framework

- **JOBS Act (Jumpstart Our Business Startups Act of 2012, Pub. L. No. 112-106)** — the full statutory text; the Title I amendments to the Securities Act and Exchange Act that created the EGC on-ramp. <https://www.congress.gov/bill/112th-congress/house-bill/3606>
- **SEC — JOBS Act reference page** — SEC-side materials, C&DIs, and rule releases implementing the JOBS Act. <https://www.sec.gov/spotlight/jobs-act.shtml>
- **FAST Act (Fixing America's Surface Transportation Act of 2015)** — additional EGC accommodations including specific interim-financial-statement omissions permitted for calendar-year-end issuers filing near a quarter boundary. <https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/house-bill/22>
- **SEC Rule 163B (2019)** — extension of the JOBS Act Section 5(d) testing-the-waters privilege to non-EGC issuers. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2019/33-10699.pdf>

## Sarbanes-Oxley and PCAOB

- **Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (Pub. L. No. 107-204)** — the specific statutory text; Sections 302 (CEO/CFO civil certification), 404(a) (management ICFR assessment), 404(b) (auditor attestation), 906 (18 U.S.C. §1350 criminal certification), 407 (audit-committee financial expert), 806 (whistleblower protection). <https://www.congress.gov/bill/107th-congress/house-bill/3763>
- **PCAOB — Auditing Standards** — the specific standards applicable to audits of US public-company issuers. Key standards: AS 1101 (audit risk), AS 1301 (communications with audit committees), AS 2201 (audit of internal control over financial reporting integrated with financial-statement audit), AS 2410 (related parties), AS 3101 (auditor's report including Critical Audit Matters), AS 4105 (reviews of interim financial information). <https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards>
- **PCAOB — Inspection Reports** — Part I.A / Part I.B findings for registered audit firms, useful as a quality signal in the auditor-selection process. <https://pcaobus.org/oversight/inspections>
- **SEC — Commission Guidance Regarding Management's Report on Internal Control Over Financial Reporting Under Section 13(a) or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (2007)** — the specific SEC guidance on the top-down risk-based approach to management's SOX 404(a) assessment. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/interp/2007/33-8810.pdf>
- **COSO — Internal Control — Integrated Framework (2013)** — the near-universal control framework adopted by US public issuers for the SOX 404 assessment. <https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic>

## Exchange listing standards

- **Nasdaq Listing Rules — Rule 5000 series** — the specific initial-listing standards (5405 Global Select, 5505 Global, 5555 Capital Market) and continuing-listing standards (5450 series), plus the Rule 5600 corporate-governance requirements (5605 board and committees, 5608 clawback, 5610 code of conduct, 5615 controlled-company and phase-in transitions, 5250 disclosure of insider-trading policy). <https://listingcenter.nasdaq.com/rulebook/nasdaq/rules>
- **NYSE Listed Company Manual** — the specific initial-listing standards (§102), continued-listing standards (§802), and corporate-governance requirements (§303A subsections including .01 majority-independent, .02 independence definition, .03 executive sessions, .04 nominating and corporate governance committee, .05 compensation committee, .06 and .07 audit committee, .10 code of business conduct, .14 clawback). <https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/regulation/nyse/NYSE_Listed_Company_Manual.pdf>
- **SEC Rule 10A-3** — the SEC rule requiring listed-company audit-committee independence, applicable to both NYSE and Nasdaq. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/subject-group-ECFRc169fa4d64a2eee/section-240.10A-3>

## Recent SEC rulemakings referenced in this module

- **SEC Item 105 Risk Factor Amendments (2020, Release Nos. 33-10825, 34-89670)** — the specific move to a principles-based standard, plain-English requirement, and 15-page soft cap for risk-factor disclosure. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2020/33-10825.pdf>
- **SEC Item 303 MD&A Amendments (2020, Release Nos. 33-10890, 34-90459)** — the modernised MD&A framework. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2021/33-10890.pdf>
- **SEC Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure (2023, Release Nos. 33-11216, 34-97989)** — the specific Item 106 of Reg S-K and Form 8-K Item 1.05 disclosure obligations. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2023/33-11216.pdf>
- **SEC Pay Versus Performance Disclosure (2022, Release Nos. 34-95607)** — Item 402(v) of Reg S-K. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2022/34-95607.pdf>
- **SEC Rule 10b5-1 and Insider Trading Amendments (2022, Release Nos. 33-11138, 34-96492)** — the 2023-effective cooling-off periods, self-certification, overlapping-plan and single-trade-plan limits, and Form 10-Q / 10-K disclosure. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2022/33-11138.pdf>

- **SEC Climate-Related Disclosure Rule (2024, Release Nos. 33-11275, 34-99678)** — the specific climate-related disclosure requirements subject to specific litigation and specific stay-and-remand posture; check current status before relying on specific implementation timing. <https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2024/33-11275.pdf> <!-- needs-research: current implementation status of the 2024 climate disclosure rule after the SEC's 2024 stay -->
