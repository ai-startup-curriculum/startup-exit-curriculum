# Resources for mod-101-endgame-strategy-and-optionality-design

Primary source documentation, practitioner canon, data providers, and legal / accounting references used by this module. Prefer these over secondary write-ups: they are authoritative, and — for the data providers especially — the sources you cite in a board memo need to be sources the board can re-check next quarter.

## Practitioner canon — endgame framing

The specific analytic frames in chapters 1, 2, and 6 draw on this small set of writing. All of it is worth reading directly.

- **Elad Gil — *High Growth Handbook*** — board chapters (composition, working with your board through hard decisions), the M&A chapter (thinking about acquisition as a founder-CEO), the general hire-ahead-of-the-curve pattern. <https://growth.eladgil.com/>
- **Ben Horowitz — *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*** — especially "Should You Sell Your Company?" chapter (the closest single-chapter treatment of the endgame-decision frame in the canon). <https://www.benhorowitz.com/the-hard-thing-about-hard-things>
- **Peter Thiel — *Zero to One*, Chapter 14 ("The Founder's Paradox")** — founder-personal-brand considerations inside the fiduciary decision. <https://zerotoonebook.com/>
- **Reid Hoffman — *Blitzscaling* (book) and *Masters of Scale* essays** — stage-of-blitzscaling framing that maps onto how the endgame frame is stable across stages but sensitive at inflection points. <https://www.mastersofscale.com/>
- **Brad Feld and Jason Mendelson — *Venture Deals*** — term-sheet mechanics that the operating audit in chapter 5 depends on. <https://feld.com/books/venture-deals>
- **Brad Feld, Matt Blumberg, Mahendra Ramsinghani — *Startup Boards*** — board composition, board work, and the founder-board relationship. <https://feld.com/books/startup-boards>
- **Fred Wilson — AVC blog (ongoing)** — the M&A and IPO essays; use as periodic reading to keep the frame honest against practitioner reality. <https://avc.com/>
- **First Round Review** — endgame, board, and CEO-succession essays. <https://review.firstround.com/>
- **Andy Rachleff — "Product-Market Fit" and later writing on stay-independent vs. exit** — Wealthfront / Benchmark alumnus writing on the discipline of pointing the operating loop at a chosen endgame. <https://blog.wealthfront.com/> (search author)
- **a16z — Podcast and essays on M&A and endgame** — mixed depth but useful for practitioner-current framing. <https://a16z.com/podcast/>

## IPO-window and public-comparables data

- **Meritech Capital — public comparables** — SaaS and adjacent public-company comps with sector-multiple readings updated regularly. Widely used by growth-equity and IPO-track practitioners as an IPO-window anchor. <https://www.meritechcapital.com/public-comparables>
- **Bessemer Venture Partners — BVP Nasdaq Emerging Cloud Index (NQCLOUD, the Bessemer Cloud Index)** — curated basket of cloud-software public companies with historical multiple / growth / rule-of-40 data. <https://cloudindex.bvp.com/>
- **Renaissance Capital — IPO Center** — IPO calendar, first-day performance data, aftermarket performance by cohort. <https://www.renaissancecapital.com/ipo-center/stats>
- **S&P Biotech Index (XBI, iShares S&P Biotech ETF)** — sector-index anchor for biotech-window reads. <https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239699/> (ticker XBI)
- **Global X FinTech ETF (FINX)** — sector-index anchor for fintech-window reads. <https://www.globalxetfs.com/funds/finx/>
- **Nasdaq — listing standards** — the current NYSE and Nasdaq listing-standard governance requirements referenced when discussing IPO-readiness governance graduation (referenced in mod-107 depth). Nasdaq: <https://listingcenter.nasdaq.com/rulebook/nasdaq/rules>. NYSE: <https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/regulation/nyse/NYSE_Listed_Company_Manual.pdf>

## M&A market data

- **PitchBook** — subscription. Private-market and M&A activity data, valuations, deal-terms studies. <https://pitchbook.com/>
- **LSEG (formerly Refinitiv) — Deals Intelligence M&A League Tables** — quarterly M&A league tables by advisor and sector, publicly available in press-release form. <https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/products/deals-intelligence>
- **Mergermarket** — subscription. M&A intelligence with early-signal reporting. <https://www.mergermarket.com/>

- **SRS Acquiom — M&A deal-terms studies** — practitioner-level annual studies of escrow, indemnification, R&W-insurance, and earn-out terms based on transactions SRS Acquiom administers. <https://www.srsacquiom.com/resources/reports/>

## Filings and regulatory sources

- **SEC EDGAR** — the source of truth for confidential and public S-1 filings, DRS submissions, 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K disclosures, and M&A-related tender-offer materials. Search by company or by filing type. <https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search-and-access>
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance — reference materials** — for the IPO-readiness and disclosure framework. <https://www.sec.gov/divisions/corpfin>
- **SEC — Rule 10b-5 and Section 10(b)** — the fraud and disclosure liability that shapes public-company communications discipline. Full statutes: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/78j> (Section 10(b)); Rule 10b-5: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.10b-5>
- **JOBS Act (Emerging Growth Company framework)** — SEC materials on EGC election and scaled-disclosure. <https://www.sec.gov/spotlight/jobs-act.shtml>

## Delaware fiduciary framework (background for chapters 5–6 and depth in mod-109)

- **Delaware Court of Chancery** — recent M&A opinions on fiduciary duty, Corwin cleansing, MFW dual protection, entire-fairness review, Van Gorkom informed-decision, Blasius franchise-interference, Revlon change-of-control. <https://courts.delaware.gov/chancery/>
- **Delaware General Corporation Law (DGCL)** — the underlying corporate-law framework. <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/index.shtml>
- **DGCL §262 — appraisal rights** — the statutory right that shapes fiduciary and process discipline in transactions. <https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc09/index.shtml>
- **Weil / Fried Frank / Wachtell / Cravath M&A memos** — leading corporate-law firm publications on recent Delaware case law. Wachtell's "Takeover Response Checklist" and Fried Frank's M/A Quarterly Newsletter are widely-referenced. Wachtell: <https://www.wlrk.com/publications/> Fried Frank: <https://www.friedfrank.com/insights>

## NVCA / model documents (background for chapter 5)

- **NVCA Model Legal Documents** — the industry-standard preferred-stock financing document set (charter, stockholders' agreement, voting agreement, drag-along, protective provisions). The operating audit in chapter 5 reasons about departures from and modifications to these baseline documents. <https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/>
- **Cooley GO — Legal Documents Library** — practitioner templates and commentary for venture financings, employment agreements, and commercial contracts. <https://www.cooleygo.com/documents/>
- **Latham & Watkins — Book of Jargon (Venture Capital)** — the shared vocabulary reference. Book of Jargon series: <https://www.lw.com/en/book-of-jargon-series>

## Tax framework (background — depth in mod-103 and mod-114)

- **IRC §368 — reorganizations (A / B / C / F reorg types)** — the tax framework for stock-based M&A consideration. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/368>
- **IRC §338(h)(10) — election to treat stock deal as asset deal** — often relevant for S-corp targets. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/338>
- **IRC §280G — golden parachute** — the change-of-control executive-comp framework. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/280G>
- **IRC §409A — nonqualified deferred compensation** — payment-timing constraints that shape earn-outs and transaction bonuses. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/409A>
- **IRC §1202 — Qualified Small Business Stock exclusion** — the founder / early-employee tax treatment preserved (or forfeit) by transaction structure. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/1202>

## Antitrust and regulatory-clearance framework (background — depth in mod-103)

- **Hart-Scott-Rodino (HSR) Antitrust Improvements Act — Premerger Notification Program (FTC)** — the reporting threshold, filing-fee tiers, and waiting-period mechanics that shape M&A timing. Current thresholds are updated annually by the FTC. <https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program>
- **Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS)** — the national-security review that applies to transactions involving a foreign acquirer and sensitive US-business definitions (TID US business). <https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/international/the-committee-on-foreign-investment-in-the-united-states-cfius>
- **DOJ Antitrust Division — press releases and enforcement actions** — for tracking sector-relevant enforcement posture. <https://www.justice.gov/atr>

## Post-close integration and succession canon (background — depth in mod-111 and mod-112)

- **Mark Sirower — *The Synergy Trap* (1997) and *The Synergy Solution* (2022)** — the empirical case that most acquisitions destroy value and the framework for the minority that do not. <https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=1998> (The Synergy Trap)
- **HBR — CEO Succession research (Charan and others)** — the canonical framing of succession as an ongoing board discipline. <https://hbr.org/topic/succession-planning>
- **Bolster — Board and executive-bench insights** — practitioner writing on independent-director sourcing and executive-bench design. <https://bolster.com/insights>

## Wind-down and personal-wealth references (background — depth in mod-113 and mod-114)

- **NVCA + Cooley — Guide to Winding Down Your Startup** — practitioner reference for the shutdown decision framework. <https://nvca.org/resource/nvca-model-legal-documents/>
- **US Department of Labor — WARN Act materials** — the layoff-notification framework that applies in most shutdowns and in some transaction contexts. <https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/layoffs/warn>
- **IRC §6672 — trust-fund taxes** — officer personal-liability watch that applies in shutdowns. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6672>
- **Carta — Post-Liquidity Planning and Equity Learning Center** — practitioner primer on personal-wealth-planning post-transaction. <https://carta.com/learn/personal-finance/>
- **Holloway Guide to Equity Compensation** — thorough reference on the equity-comp instruments and their treatment in an exit. <https://www.holloway.com/g/equity-compensation>

## Ownership boundary — where the depth lives

This module frames the endgame decision. It does not re-teach the underlying mechanics. When a specific mechanic is needed:

- **Cap-table maintenance, waterfall math, preferred-stock economics, 409A methodology** → `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum` mod-104 (equity economics) and mod-106 (valuation).
- **Startup-as-a-system model, stage ladder, founder-numbers slice, founder's operating loop** → `startup-foundations`.
- **Ongoing-company board and committee construction, corporate-secretary machinery, employment-contract suite, commercial-contract library** → `startup-operations-governance-curriculum`.
- **Deep sell-side M&A execution (banker selection, CIM, teaser, buyer-mapping at scoring depth, pre-marketing choreography)** → mod-102 in this track.
- **Deep M&A deal-structure design (asset vs. stock, reverse-triangular, earn-out design, escrow, tax structuring, HSR / CFIUS)** → mod-103 in this track.
- **Deep IPO-readiness and execution mechanics (S-1 authoring, PCAOB audit, SOX ICFR, JOBS Act EGC, bookbuild, direct listing, SPAC)** → mod-107 and mod-108 in this track.
- **Deep transaction-governance and fiduciary-duty analysis (Revlon, Corwin, MFW, entire-fairness review, special-committee construction, fairness opinions, ISS / Glass Lewis engagement)** → mod-109 in this track.
- **Secondary-transaction mechanics (founder secondary sizing, tender-offer design, forward-contract secondaries, ROFR choreography)** → mod-106 in this track.
- **Wind-down mechanics (ABC, Ch 7 vs. Ch 11, Section 363, WARN Act shutdown, officer personal-liability watch)** → mod-113 in this track.

> Data-provider URLs and specific numeric thresholds (HSR filing thresholds, tax code phase-ins, IPO pricing norms) update at least annually and sometimes mid-year. When an exercise or memo cites a specific value, follow the link, snapshot the URL and date in your notes, and use that; do not rely on numbers that may be stale in this document.
