# Startup Exit & Endgame Curriculum

**Role level:** 60 (deepest and latest-stage functional pillar — Startup family)
**Family:** Startup
**Status:** planned — modules and projects below are the planned scope authored from [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Lessons and worked exemplars will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles, oldest-gap first.

## Overview

The deepest and latest-stage functional pillar of the startup family — the **transaction-execution-and-endgame-decision** apparatus that converts an ongoing company into a realised outcome: acquisition, initial public offering, secondary liquidity, leadership succession, or responsible shutdown. Endgame Strategy & Optionality Design → M&A Market Structure & Acquirer Motivations → M&A Deal Structures & Consideration → LOI / Negotiation / Definitive Agreements → M&A Diligence & Data-Room Operations → Secondary Transactions & Pre-Exit Liquidity → IPO Readiness & Dual-Track Preparation → IPO Execution (Roadshow, Pricing, Alternatives) → Transaction Governance & Fiduciary Duty → Transaction Communications & Stakeholder Management → Post-Close Integration & 100-Day Plan → Succession & Leadership Transitions → Winding Down (Shutdown Mechanics & Wind-Down Duties) → Post-Transaction Personal & Wealth Planning.

The fourteen modules equip a founder-CEO, CFO, GC, SVP-Corp-Dev, or investment-banker to reason about the endgame decision (*sell, IPO, secondaries, stay independent, or wind down — and why?*), execute a full sell-side or buy-side transaction (*banker selection, buyer-mapping, LOI negotiation, definitive-agreement authoring, diligence workstreams, R&W insurance, tax structuring, HSR / CFIUS clearance*), run a dual-track IPO + strategic-sale process (*S-1 authoring, PCAOB audit, SOX readiness, JOBS Act EGC election, underwriter bake-off, roadshow choreography, pricing, greenshoe, lock-ups, direct-listing / SPAC / Reg A+ alternatives*), maintain the transaction-governance discipline that survives a stockholder challenge (*Revlon / Corwin / MFW analysis, special-committee construction, fairness-opinion procurement, banker-conflict discipline under Rural Metro, ISS / Glass Lewis engagement, stockholder-litigation defence*), manage the human side of a transaction (*employee choreography pre-and-post-signing, customer / partner / press strategy, MIP design at IRC 280G / 409A guardrails, WARN Act compliance*), run first-100-day integration (*IMO construction, technology-and-systems decisions, culture integration, retention monitoring, escrow-release and earn-out management*), execute leadership succession (*founder-CEO to professional-CEO handoff, board-chair transitions, executive-team bench, board-composition graduation*), wind down a startup responsibly (*Assignment for the Benefit of Creditors, Chapter 7 vs. Chapter 11, WARN, D&O tail, officer personal-liability watch under IRC §6672 / PAGA / FLSA / ERISA*), and close the endgame arc with post-transaction personal-wealth planning (*IRC 1202 QSBS, QSBS stacking, Rule 10b5-1 trading plans, exchange funds, CRT / DAF / private-foundation structuring, family-office setup*). Total planned commitment: **318 hours across 14 modules** + **112 hours across 3 projects** = **~430 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the transaction-execution-and-endgame-decision depth for founder-CEO / CFO / GC / SVP-Corp-Dev / IB-MD seats — Endgame Strategy & Optionality Design, M&A Market Structure & Acquirer Motivations, M&A Deal Structures & Consideration Design, LOI / Negotiation / Definitive Agreements, M&A Diligence & Data-Room Operations, Secondary Transactions & Pre-Exit Liquidity, IPO Readiness & Dual-Track Preparation, IPO Execution — Roadshow / Pricing / Alternatives, Transaction Governance & Fiduciary Duty, Transaction Communications & Stakeholder Management, Post-Close Integration & 100-Day Plan, Succession & Leadership Transitions, Winding Down — Shutdown Mechanics & Wind-Down Duties, and Post-Transaction Personal & Wealth Planning.
- **Defers down** to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the startup-as-a-system model, the dependency graph, the stage axis (IDEA → PRE-SEED → SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE), the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice. This track *never* re-teaches those; it layers endgame depth on top and only becomes actionable at or approaching GROWTH / MATURE.
- **Defers sideways** to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) (level 40) for the ongoing-company financial infrastructure (three-statement model, cohort unit economics, cap-table maintenance, priced-round preferred-stock economics, CFO-side board packs, monthly close, finance-org design) and for equity *economics* (waterfall analysis under preference stacks, 409A methodology, Rule 701 aggregate-value caps); to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) (level 50) for the ongoing-company People / Legal / Operations / Governance infrastructure (this track owns the transaction-specific overlays: special-committee formation, transaction-committee construction, transaction-D&O tail, transaction-communications discipline, transaction-retention plans, transaction-workforce actions under WARN, IPO-readiness NYSE / Nasdaq listing-standard governance graduation); to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) (level 30) for the ongoing-company GTM infrastructure inspected during buy-side commercial diligence.
- **Defers sideways / up** to the role-pathway repos ([`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum), and future `cfo-curriculum` / `coo-curriculum` / `general-counsel-curriculum` / `head-of-corporate-development-curriculum`) that inherit this curriculum at their declared coverage and add role-specific curated views — no fork, no duplication.
- **Defers cross-family** to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) for the security-engineering content of buy-side diligence, and to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) / [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) for the AI-safety-technical content of buy-side diligence and the AI-vendor-diligence checkpoints.

Below this pillar sit future sub-tracks (`startup-corporate-development-analyst`, `startup-restructuring-controllership`) that would own operator-mechanic depth (deal modelling, diligence workstream execution, ABC petition preparation) that a full-time corp-dev analyst or restructuring specialist would experience — this pillar covers those at the founder-CEO / CFO / GC / SVP-Corp-Dev decision level rather than the operator-mechanic level.

See [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements-to-coverage map and the cited public references the catalog is grounded in. See [`PREREQUISITES.md`](PREREQUISITES.md) for the assumed lower-level tracks and working knowledge.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — Endgame Strategy & Optionality Design
Frame the endgame decision at the earliest stage a founder can afford to — what "success" means (acquisition, IPO, secondary liquidity, stay-independent, responsible shutdown), the trade-offs among them, and the operating-decision consequences of each frame. Maintain a rolling buyer map through the ongoing-company lifecycle. Track market-window signals (IPO-window indicators from Meritech / Bessemer, M&A-market volume from PitchBook / Mergermarket, sector-multiple compression, strategic-buyer appetite). Design operating decisions upstream so endgame optionality remains live — financing choices, cap-table structure, board composition, contract terms. Author an endgame-optionality memo grounded in Elad Gil / Reid Hoffman / Ben Horowitz / Peter Thiel / Brad Feld canon.

### mod-102 — M&A Market Structure & Acquirer Motivations
Strategic-vs-financial acquirer typology and the aqui-hire / tuck-in / platform / transformative typology. A buyer-mapping methodology (relationship depth × strategic fit × ability-to-pay × integration-appetite × process-behaviour × cultural fit). The investment-bank engagement decision (sole vs. dual mandate, tier-1 vs. boutique, retainer + success-fee economics). The pre-marketing document set (teaser, CIM, management presentation) and the choreography that seeds a competitive process without appearing shopped. Grounded in Rosenbaum-Pearl, Goldman TMT / Morgan Stanley TMT / Qatalyst / Centerview canon, and Cooley / WSGR / Orrick practitioner materials.

### mod-103 — M&A Deal Structures & Consideration Design
Asset vs. stock vs. reverse-triangular / forward-triangular / forward merger vs. spin-merge (Reverse Morris Trust) form selection. Consideration mix design (cash vs. stock vs. mixed vs. CVRs / seller-notes / rollover equity). Earn-out design (target, cliff, acceleration, control-and-oversight, dispute resolution). Escrow / holdback / working-capital adjustment mechanics against ABA Deal Points Study benchmarks. Tax structuring under IRC §368 (A / B / C / F reorg) vs. §338(h)(10) election. IRC §280G golden-parachute analysis and mitigation. IRC §409A payment-timing constraints. IRC §1202 QSBS preservation through the transaction. HSR / CFIUS / foreign-antitrust filing implications.

### mod-104 — LOI, Negotiation & Definitive Agreements
Author binding-vs-nonbinding term-sheet architecture. Negotiate exclusivity / no-shop / standstill / expense-reimbursement / break-up fee / matching rights against ABA / WSGR benchmarks. Auction management (single-track vs. dual-track vs. limited-round vs. broad). Draft the definitive agreements (SPA / Merger Agreement / APA) with reps-and-warranties layering, interim-operating covenants, closing conditions, MAC / MAE definition negotiation. Design the indemnification package (survival, cap, basket, tipping, de-minimis, sole-and-exclusive-remedy, sandbagging language). Structure R&W insurance vs. traditional indemnification. Apply Freund / Ury / Fisher / Voss negotiation canon at deal-scale.

### mod-105 — M&A Diligence & Data-Room Operations
Construct a sell-side data room across all workstreams with defensible architecture, redaction discipline, clean-team protocols, and virtual-data-room selection (Intralinks / Datasite / Firmex / Ansarada / DealRoom). Prepare sell-side quality-of-earnings with EBITDA-normalisation adjustments that survive rebuttal. Manage sell-side diligence response with workstream owners and SLA discipline. Design a buy-side diligence workstream across financial / legal / tax / commercial / technology / IP / HR / privacy / security / AI-model / open-source. Drive diligence-findings-based price renegotiation. Negotiate R&W underwriter scope. Recognises the security-engineering / AI-safety-technical boundaries with `security-learning` and `chief-ai-officer-learning` / `head-of-ai-governance-learning`.

### mod-106 — Secondary Transactions & Pre-Exit Liquidity
Founder secondaries — sizing, board-approval choreography, tax treatment, signalling-management. Employee tender-offer design — pricing, participation caps, waterfall analysis across the preference stack, tax withholding, operational execution. Forward-contract structured secondaries and counterparty-credit-risk analysis. Secondary-market platform execution (Nasdaq Private Market, Forge, EquityZen, Carta X, PARAGON Prime) with FINRA Notice 09-05 and Section 4(a)(7) / Rule 144 / 144A compliance. ROFR and co-sale choreography. Tender-in-connection-with-financing pattern. 409A refresh interaction. Recognises the equity *economics* boundary with `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`.

### mod-107 — IPO Readiness & Dual-Track Preparation
The going-public decision framework (ARR, growth, Rule of 40, gross margin, NRR, governance readiness, market-window). S-1 authoring across business, risk factors, MD&A, executive-comp CD&A, financial statements. PCAOB-registered auditor engagement and 2-year audit history preparation. SOX 404(a) / 404(b) internal-control readiness with ICFR design and testing. JOBS Act EGC election and scaled-disclosure choices. Dual-track M&A + IPO governance choreography. NYSE / Nasdaq listing-standard governance graduation. EDGAR filing workflow. Recognises the ongoing-company finance-function boundary with `startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum` mod-111.

### mod-108 — IPO Execution — Roadshow, Pricing & Alternatives
Underwriter bake-off and selection (lead-left / joint-lead / co-managers, tier-1 vs. sector-boutique, gross-spread negotiation, syndicate construction). TTW and gun-jumping compliance under the JOBS Act. Roadshow choreography (pilot fishing, cornerstone conversations, mega-fund one-on-ones, T-14 to T-1 pricing week). Pricing and allocation methodology. Regulation M stabilisation and greenshoe over-allotment. Lock-up architecture with staggered releases and carve-outs. IPO alternatives — direct listing (SEC Rule 5315 / NYSE 102.01B), SPAC merger and de-SPAC (post-2024 SEC-rules mechanics), Direct Public Offering, Reg A+ Tier 2 — with a decision framework for choosing among them.

### mod-109 — Transaction Governance & Fiduciary Duty
Apply the Delaware fiduciary-duty framework at deal-scale — business-judgement baseline, Van Gorkom / Unocal / Revlon / Weinberger / Blasius standards. Apply the Corwin business-judgement gate and the MFW / Kahn v. M&F Worldwide dual-protection framework. Construct a special committee with independent-and-empowered composition, advisor selection, and process discipline. Procure a fairness opinion with banker-conflict analysis under Rural Metro / Del Monte / El Paso. Assess appraisal-rights exposure under DGCL §262. Engage ISS and Glass Lewis for M&A and IPO-governance votes. Design stockholder-litigation defence with exclusive-forum bylaws, D&O tail policies, and plaintiff-firm engagement pattern recognition.

### mod-110 — Transaction Communications & Stakeholder Management
Pre-signing employee-communications choreography (need-to-know discipline, deal-team clean list). Post-signing employee choreography (day-of sequencing, manager one-on-one waves, retention conversations, benefits-transition FAQ, integration-day-one messaging). Customer / partner / channel-reseller communications with pre-close consent obligations. Press and analyst strategy against Reg FD, quiet-period compliance for IPOs, activist / short-seller / journalist inbound handling. Management-incentive-plan (MIP) design at IRC 280G / 409A guardrails, executive retention agreements, single-vs-double-trigger acceleration. WARN Act compliance including state mini-WARNs. Works-council and national-security notifications.

### mod-111 — Post-Close Integration & 100-Day Plan
Construct an Integration Management Office (IMO). Design a first-100-day plan for the acquired company (day-1 readiness → day-30 systems cutover → day-90 org integration → day-100 review). Design a first-100-day plan for the founder-inside-acquirer seat with reporting-line clarity, decision-rights division, and the honest-signal pattern for a seat that is not working. Make technology-and-systems integration decisions (consolidate vs. preserve, engineering-org merge, data migration, security-perimeter consolidation). Design culture integration (values mapping, comp-and-leveling harmonisation, benefits harmonisation, ritual preservation). Monitor retention against the retention-plan design. Manage escrow-release, earn-out achievement, and working-capital true-up. Grounded in Sirower / McKinsey / BCG / Bain / Big-Four PMI canon.

### mod-112 — Succession & Leadership Transitions
Read founder-CEO succession readiness signals (operating scale, founder-fit-with-current-stage, board-composition, funding-stage, founder-personal-readiness). Choreograph the board conversation on succession with executive-search partner selection (Heidrick / Spencer Stuart / True Search / ON Partners / DHR / Daversa / Bolster). Design the incoming-CEO 30/60/90/180-day onboarding programme. Design the founder-transition-to-Chair / Chief Innovation Officer / Chief Product Officer role scoping. Manage board-chair transitions (independent-chair vs. combined-chair-and-CEO decision). Design president / COO succession, executive-team succession bench with nine-box calibration, and the interim-CEO playbook. Manage board-composition changes at growth / mature stage (VC-seat sunset to observer, public-company-director additions, activism-defence composition).

### mod-113 — Winding Down — Shutdown Mechanics & Wind-Down Duties
Apply the shutdown-vs-sale-for-parts-vs-Assignment-for-the-Benefit-of-Creditors-vs-Chapter-7-vs-Chapter-11 decision framework. Execute an ABC (Sherwood Partners / Development Specialists Inc. / SierraConstellation / Armanino / FTI assignee selection, assignment-agreement mechanics, priority-of-claims waterfall, California CCP §493 / Delaware Chapter 15 alternative). Analyse Chapter 7 vs. Chapter 11 (Section 363 sales, pre-packaged Ch 11, Chapter 15 cross-border). Manage WARN Act compliance in a shutdown with faltering-company / unforeseeable-circumstance exception analysis. Manage employee obligations (final pay, PTO payout, COBRA, severance, non-compete enforceability post-2024 FTC ban). Manage IP disposition (sell / assign / abandon / license-back). Manage investor communications with LP-report obligations. Purchase D&O tail (6-year run-off). Watch officer personal-liability under IRC §6672 (trust-fund taxes), PAGA, WARN, FLSA / state wage-and-hour, and ERISA.

### mod-114 — Post-Transaction Personal & Wealth Planning
Apply IRC §1202 QSBS exclusion — 5-year holding, original-issuance, $50M gross-asset test, $10M / 10x-basis per-issuer cap, state conformity variation (CA / PA non-conforming). Structure QSBS stacking with non-grantor trusts, gifting-to-family-members, and IRC §1045 rollover. Author a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan for a newly-public founder against the SEC 2023 amendments (90-120 day cooling-off, self-certification, no-overlapping-plans). Design a post-lockup diversification strategy (staged sale, exchange-fund alternatives, options collars, prepaid variable forward contracts). Structure charitable-remainder-trust / donor-advised-fund / private-foundation vehicles. Set up a family office (direct vs. multi-family vs. outsourced-CIO decision, staffing pattern, technology stack, governance model). This module delivers education, not personal tax / investment / legal advice, and hands off to qualified counsel.
