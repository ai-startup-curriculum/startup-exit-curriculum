# Startup Exit & Endgame Curriculum

> **How do I steer a company to an outcome — acquisition, IPO, succession, or a
> clean shutdown?**

A functional / topic curriculum at the far end of the
[dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md)
— it draws on Growth, Governance, Finance, and Equity, and only becomes
actionable at the later stages. Start with
[startup-foundations](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations).

## Modules

| Module | Stage | What you'll be able to do |
|---|---|---|
| `mod-001-endgame-strategy` | GROWTH | Reason about outcomes early: what "success" means and how it shapes decisions |
| `mod-002-ma-and-acquisitions` | GROWTH→MATURE | Understand acquirer motivations, deal structures, diligence, and earn-outs |
| `mod-003-secondaries-and-liquidity` | GROWTH | Founder/employee liquidity before an exit; secondary sales and their trade-offs |
| `mod-004-ipo-readiness` | MATURE | What going public actually requires — governance, reporting, timelines |
| `mod-005-succession-and-leadership-transition` | MATURE | CEO succession, board transitions, founder role changes |
| `mod-006-winding-down-well` | any | Shutting down responsibly: obligations to employees, investors, customers |

## Stage note

Most of this is **premature before GROWTH** — it is stage-tagged so earlier-stage
founders can safely defer it (see
[STARTUP_STAGES](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md)).
