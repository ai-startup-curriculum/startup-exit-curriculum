The deepest and latest-stage functional pillar of the Startup family — the **transaction-execution-and-endgame-decision** apparatus that converts an ongoing company into a realised outcome: **Endgame Strategy → M&A Market Structure → M&A Deal Structures → LOI / Negotiation / Definitive Agreements → M&A Diligence & Data-Room Operations → Secondary Transactions → IPO Readiness & Dual-Track → IPO Execution → Transaction Governance & Fiduciary Duty → Transaction Communications → Post-Close Integration → Succession → Winding Down → Post-Transaction Personal Wealth**. Covers the founder-CEO / CFO / GC / SVP-Corp-Dev / IB-MD seats through acquisition, IPO, secondary liquidity, leadership succession, or responsible shutdown.

> **Status**: planned. Curriculum shape authored in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json); requirements catalog in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json); role overview in [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md). Lessons and worked exemplars will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles.

## Ownership at a glance

Owns depth for the transaction-execution-and-endgame-decision branch of the startup dependency graph. Defers *down* to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the startup-as-a-system model and the founder-numbers slice; *sideways* to [`startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-finance-fundraising-curriculum) (level 40) for the ongoing-company financial infrastructure and equity *economics*; to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) (level 50) for the ongoing-company governance / people / legal / ops infrastructure (this track owns the transaction-specific overlays); and to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) (level 30) for the ongoing-company GTM infrastructure inspected during buy-side commercial diligence. Defers *up* to role pathways ([`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum), plus future `cfo-curriculum` / `coo-curriculum` / `general-counsel-curriculum` / `head-of-corporate-development-curriculum`). Defers *cross-family* to [`security-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/security-learning) for security-engineering diligence content and to [`chief-ai-officer-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/chief-ai-officer-learning) / [`head-of-ai-governance-learning`](https://github.com/ai-governance-curriculum/head-of-ai-governance-learning) for AI-safety-technical diligence content. See [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md#ownership-rule) for the full ownership rule.
